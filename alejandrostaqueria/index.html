Our Story

Real Mexican food, made the old-school way.

Alejandro's Taqueria is the kind of spot Fairfield locals point you to when you ask "where's the best birria in town?" We keep it simple and do it right — slow-braised meats, handmade tortillas, and honest portions at a fair price.

The star is our Birria — tender, slow-cooked beef served as a plate or folded into crispy, cheesy quesabirrias with a cup of rich consomé for dipping. Once you try it, you'll be back.

  • 🥩Slow-braised birria — tender and full of flavor.
  • 🫔Handmade tortillas, pressed and griddled fresh.
  • 🌮Tacos, burritos, tortas & quesadillas — your way.
  • 🦐Seafood plates: 7 Mares, camarones & more.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦Family owned and run, loved by generations.
