
## ⚠️ AOS layer feeder anomaly (cron 04:57 UTC)

The `aos-layer-feeder` cron ran fine (exit 0, added 5 identity anchors), but the consciousness layers are NOT staying healthy:

- BEFORE: Subconscious 100/100, Unconscious 776/2000
- AFTER (~1s later): Subconscious 72/100, Unconscious 9/2000
- Re-check (seconds later): Subconscious 6/100, Unconscious 13/2000

**Root cause:** In `/root/.aos/aos/consciousness_layers.py`, `ConsciousnessLayer.decay_rate = 0.01` with linear decay `intensity * (1 - age_seconds * 0.01)` in `get_active()`. This fully decays ANY item in ~100 seconds.

**Impact:** The feeder cron runs every 5 min (300s), but items decay to zero in ~100s. The "persistent" layers are empty ~98% of the time. Identity anchors (intensity ~0.96) drop below the 0.3 threshold in ~69s.

**Fix options:**
1. Lower `decay_rate` to ~0.0001 (≈2.8h horizon) or switch to exponential half-life (e.g. half-life in hours).
2. Or run the feeder far more frequently (every ~30s) — band-aid, not a real fix.
