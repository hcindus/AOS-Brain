### UPDATE 9 (01:58 UTC) — Conflict #1 RESOLVED: TCPA ≠ McKibben
Captain: "Yes" (take the pass at conflict #1). Wrote `conflict-1-muscle-supply-resolution.md`.

**Root cause found:** the AX-169F manifest's "TCPA/McKibben (pneumatic)" phrasing is a conflation. TCPA = electric (Joule-heated, silent, NO compressor); McKibben = pneumatic (needs compressor + valves + reservoir, +2–4 kg, noisy). They are different actuators.

**Resolution:** delete the compressor entirely. Since load-bearing (hips/knees/shoulders) is already electric QDD/SEA, and the muscle fraction (spine/neck/face/hand) only needs 1–3 Nm (TCPA's 1–5 N/fiber, bundled to N×, suffices), there's no case for pneumatic force in any soft region. Net: −2 to −4 kg, silent, current-only supply on the existing power bus.

**v2 path (don't build now):** electrofluidic fiber (MIT 2026, McKibben + EHD pump) re-opens pneumatic-grade force + compliance untethered, only if ever needed.

**Remaining risks:** TCPA thermal bandwidth (cooling rate is the ceiling — ties to conflict #3 spine part-count), TCPA lifetime/fatigue over 2000h, electrofluidic fiber not purchasable today.

**Budget updated:** conflict #1 marked RESOLVED in `mass-power-budget.md`. Only remaining open conflict = #3 (spine part-count / thermal bandwidth) — now the sequential next pass.
