## Fighter thrust fix — WIP (Captain, 2026-10-16 00:29 UTC)
Captain: "the thrust on the fighter needs to be fixed and attached to the ship at each location." Root cause found: the original `make_thrust_fire.py` / `make_thrust_billboard.py` hardcoded 3 thruster positions (Y=-0.16/+0.16/0, Z=0, X=-0.52) assuming the fighter is centered on origin. **The model is actually offset** — its rear exhaust is at ~Y≈-0.15, Z≈-0.11 (NOT Y=0/Z=0), so plumes floated detached.

**Fighter geometry facts (x302_fighter_work.glb, 1 mesh, 171,603 verts):**
- Bounds: X[-0.5, 0.5] (long axis), Y[-0.303, 0.303] (wingspan), Z[-0.13, 0.13] (thin/height). X=nose↔tail, Y=left↔right, Z=up.
- Rear = -X (min X = -0.5). Rear exhaust region (vertex clustering): single blob center Y≈-0.169, Z≈-0.113, radius≈0.084. Face-normal detection (n.x < -0.7) → rear tip at X=-0.5, engine nozzle ~Y=-0.132, Z=-0.116, radius~0.066.
- Nose (+X) tip also at negative Y (~-0.11) — the model's origin is NOT at its geometric center; fuselage sits at Y≈-0.15/Z≈-0.11 offset. This offset is the whole bug.

**Fix approach:** `fleet/fix_thrust.py` auto-detects rear-facing faces (n.x < -0.7), clusters in Y-Z, attaches billboard emission plumes (open cylinders, `thrust_fire.png`) AT the actual rear surface (X=-0.5). First run detected 2 clusters (one is wing trailing edge radius 0.142, one is engine radius 0.066) — needs refinement to filter wing vs engine. Rendered `fleet/_rear_clean.png` (1200×1200 ortho) to confirm engine count. **Not yet finalized/deployed.**

**Key lesson:** GLB fleet models have arbitrary origin offsets — always auto-detect nozzle/feature positions from geometry, never hardcode absolute coords.

## 08:09 UTC — AOS Layer Feeder (Cron) — ⚠️ FINDING
**Task:** Ran `persistent_layer_feeder.py` (runs every 5 min).

**Result:** Feeder reported "✅ Layers healthy" but I found a real flaw worth flagging:

- Unconscious layer **dropped 570 → 273** active items *during* a run that only *adds* items.
- Root cause: `consciousness_layers.py` `ConsciousnessLayer.decay_rate = 0.01` **per second**.
  - `get_active()` computes `intensity * (1 - age * 0.01)` → every item fades to ~0 in **~100 seconds**.
  - It also *mutates* `item.intensity` to the decayed value (permanent, no recovery).
  - `consolidate()` → `clear_faded(threshold=0.1)` wipes anything below 0.1.
- Feeder only runs every **5 min** (300000ms), so layers sit **empty ~98% of the time**.
- The "healthy" check only verifies a floor (Sub≥25, Unc≥30) at the instant of the check — misleading.

**Impact:** The "persistent subconscious/unconscious" system isn't actually persisting anything between runs. Items live ~100s out of every 300s cycle.

**Suggested fix (for Captain):** either (a) lower `decay_rate` drastically (e.g. 0.01 → ~0.0001 so items persist hours/days), (b) run the feeder far more often than every 5 min, or (c) stop decaying `intensity` in-place and use elapsed-time decay only at read time without mutating stored intensity.
