## Yann LeCun transcript — "world models over LLMs" (Captain, 2026-10-06)
Captain shared a ~27min video transcript on LeCun's thesis. Distilled:

- **Core claim (since 2022):** LLMs won't reach human-level AI — they lack a *world model*. Moravec paradox: perception/motor (easy for us, hard for machines) is the missing piece; next-token prediction stays in "discrete text world."
- **The alternative — JEPA (Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture):** predict in abstract representation space, ignore noisy detail, learn common sense from video/sensor data (like a baby dropping things to learn gravity). "World models over language models."
- **Evidence:** V-JEPA2 (1M hrs video → 65-80% robot pick/place after only 62 hrs unlabeled fine-tune); a 15M-param world model that self-learned physics (position/velocity, flags teleportation as "surprise") and plans 48× faster (1s vs 47s).
- **The money:** LeCun left Meta Nov 2025, founded AMI Labs — $1.03B seed (largest EU seed ever), $3.5B valuation, zero product/revenue by design. Backers: Bezos, Schmidt, Cuban, Nvidia, Samsung, Toyota. CEO Alexandre Lebrun.
- **Consensus shift:** Altman publicly walks back ("need a new architecture, as big as Transformers"); papers on LLM *limitations* went 1-in-10 (2022) → 1-in-3 (2025). Ilia Sutskever + Fei-Fei Li (World Labs) made the same bet separately.
- **The pushback:** Hinton (his co-Turing-winner) says today's models may already have real understanding; LeCun calls that "retirement victory-lap."
