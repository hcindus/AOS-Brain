**Built (OpenSCAD → STL → Blender → GLB):** `seg_earth_station.{scad,stl,glb}` (650KB GLB, 22K facets, manifold this time).

- EXTERNALS: 4 solar wings, 4 radiator fins, 2 docking ports (end caps), 2 robotic arms, nav/warning lights.
- Material palette via Blender classify (hull/deck/room/shaft/solar/radiator/port/arm/light/window).

**Live:** `https://tappylewis.cloud/seg-earth.html` — Earth (radius 4000 at origin) + station (y=5500, long axis Y laid horizontal via rotation.z=PI/2), IIFE bundle + WebGL fallback + links to OSY-7 and the Mars segmented station.
