**Live:** `https://tappylewis.cloud/seg-earth.html` — Earth (radius 4000 at origin) + station (y=5500, long axis Y laid horizontal via rotation.z=PI/2), IIFE bundle + WebGL fallback + links to OSY-7 and the Mars segmented station. Verified headless: station (17895 bright px) + Earth (8865 blue px) both in frame.

**Key orientation note:** my parametric stations have long axis = Y in the GLB (OpenSCAD Z-up → Blender Z → glTF Y), UNLIKE the Hi3D asset (long axis = Z). Always verify bounds via three.js Box3 before assuming.
