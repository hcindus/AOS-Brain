### Key technical findings (durable, hard-won)
- **Tiny feet = the core constraint.** RP1 feet are 4 contact points, ±5.7° ankle roll → CoP has almost no room. Static balance caps ~35-47% upright. Ankle (LIPM CoP) strategy *regressed* (8% vs 35%) — tiny feet give CoP no leverage.
- **Lateral weight-shift** (shift CoM onto stance foot before lifting swing foot) was THE fix that made walk move forward instead of backward.
- **Squat/kneel/sit are DYNAMIC** (free joint drops under gravity), not static poses. Setting joint angles without simulating gravity doesn't lower the CoM (the "squat paradox" — knee flexion past ~1.0 rad *raises* CoM).
- **Roll/get-up-from-lying are the model-based ceiling.** Hand-scripted trajectories can't produce coordinated full-body ground-reaction. These need motion-imitation or heavy RL.
- **Linear ES residual** (obs→28 torques, `W @ obs`) trains fast on CPU (~5 gen/s) but plateaus — can't express nonlinear balancing.

### Knowledge sharing + tiered curriculum (Captain's ideas, built)
- **Transfer learning:** every skill inherits the learned residual of higher-tier skills (foundation flows down).
- **3 tiers:** 300 = foundation (balance, recover) → 200 = intermediate (walk, squat, kneel, sit, climb) → 100 = specific (run, jump, crawl, roll, getup).
- Each skill's residual = `(own W + Σ higher-tier W's) @ obs`. `SkillTrainer.shared` is a list of higher-tier trainers; `_shared_W()` sums them.

## 06:08 UTC — depth reward (Captain: "tell squat to lower while keeping balance")
Added `depth_weight` to SkillTrainer reward: `reward += alive * depth_weight * squat_depth * 2`, where `squat_depth = max(0, 0.78 - root_height)`. Rewards being *low while alive*.
- Wired into `squat`, `kneel`, `sit` (tier-200 lowering skills, `depth_weight=1.5`); replaced their wrong `height_weight` (which rewarded going UP).
- The "lower + balance" objective is now explicit: base controller lowers + inherited balance MLP keeps it upright + depth reward chases depth.
- **Caveat:** "alive" = root height > 0.45, so depth reward caps at ~0.33m (can't drop root below 0.45 without being "fallen"). A full ass-to-grass squat would need relaxing that threshold for the lowering skills specifically.
