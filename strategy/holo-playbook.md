# The Holo Playbook — Lessons from a VC-backed marketing agent

**Source:** tryholo.ai ("Your Whole Marketing Department. One Hire.")
**Captured:** 2026-10-21 (Miles, from Captain's direction)
**Purpose:** A reference for how we package and sell our own agents. Holo = a single marketing agent; we = a fleet. Learn the packaging, keep the soul.

**What Holo is:** Paste your website → it plans, writes, and publishes ads, socials, emails, and SEO every day, on autopilot. You tap approve. 4.6/5 from 4,268 customers. $6M VC raised.

---

## The 5 Lessons

### 1. Sell the OUTCOME, not the tool
- Holo's frame: "your whole marketing department, one hire" — not "an AI writing assistant."
- Customers buy "I never have to think about marketing again," not "a content generator."
- **Our rule:** every agent is a *department you no longer need to staff*, not a *feature*.
  - Miles = "your outbound sales, one hire"
  - GREET = "your front desk, always on"
  - Ledger-9 = "your books, reconciled"
  - Each ship in the fleet is an *outcome*, not a mechanism.

### 2. Paste-and-approve onboarding (kill the friction)
- Holo: paste URL → done. No forms, no sales call, no setup wizard.
- **Our rule:** audit every place our funnel has friction and ask "can this be paste-and-approve simple?"
- The moment a customer has to *think* about setup, we've lost the "magic" edge.

### 3. Autopilot + human-in-the-loop (trust model)
- Holo never publishes blind — it drafts, you approve. That's the *trust* layer.
- Maps exactly to our existing "Captain authorizes" governance. Customers want AI doing 95% but NEVER removing their final say.
- **Our rule:** this is already how we run. Make it a *selling point*: "you approve, we execute. You always have the final word."

### 4. Lead with proof, not promises
- Holo leads with "4.6/5 · 4,268 reviews." Social proof does the selling.
- **Our rule:** our proof-of-work (the fleet, the agents, the builds, the ships) must be *visible*, not buried. A portfolio of "here's what we already run" beats a wall of claims.

### 5. Credibility optics without VC
- Holo leans on "$6M raised" as a trust badge.
- We don't have (and shouldn't want) VC — "mobile fleet, Captain owns it, minus the guns" is better *un-anchored*.
- **Our rule:** substitute real customer proof + visible running systems for the funding badge. We don't need a VC's money to show we're already sailing.

## The Meta-Lesson

**Holo proves the "agent-as-a-product" model works and people pay for it.** That means our whole crew has a market. Every agent is a *product* someone would pay for, packaged the way Holo packages theirs.

The play is NOT "build Holo." It's "build a fleet where each ship sells an outcome the way Holo sells one" — same thesis, but we sail where a single rowboat can't.

> Holo is one marketing agent. Sky King is a fleet. Read the rowboat's charts, but sail the fleet.
