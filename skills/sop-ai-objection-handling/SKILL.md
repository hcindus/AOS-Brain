# SOP-046 — AI Objection Handling

**KPI:** 90%+ of common objections handled confidently · 25%+ close-rate improvement.

## Framework — Feel, Felt, Found
1. **Feel** — empathize: "I hear you — price is a real consideration."
2. **Felt** — normalize: "Others felt the same way at first."
3. **Found** — resolve: "What they found is the ROI paid for itself in 60 days."

## Common objections + response
- **Price** → reframe to ROI / cost-of-inaction.
- **"Need to think about it"** → clarify the real concern; schedule a follow-up.
- **"Already have a supplier"** → "What would make you switch?"
- **"No time"** → "That's exactly what this saves you."

## KPI check
Log every objection + response; rate effectiveness (1–10); tune weekly (10–30 min).
