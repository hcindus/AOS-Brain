# First $500 Action Plan

**KPI:** Land at least one paid AI-video project within 14 days.

## The 14-day plan (mechanics, not motivation)

- **Days 1–3** — Create two spec samples: a product ad + a brand explainer for two real local businesses, using their actual branding from their website. Keep each 15–30s.
- **Day 4** — Send both samples directly (email, Instagram DM, or in person): "Made this for you — no charge for the sample. If you'd like more, here's what I offer."
- **Day 5** — Create a Fiverr gig + an Upwork profile. Upload your trailer + spec samples.
- **Days 6–7** — Apply to 10 relevant Upwork posts (Path A proposal structure).
- **Days 8–14** — Follow up with local businesses. Apply to 10 more Upwork posts. Post your trailer on LinkedIn.

Most people who follow this land at least one paid project within 14 days. The first is the hardest — after that, referrals, reviews, and momentum do the heavy lifting.

## Raising prices over time
| Experience | Pricing benchmark |
|------------|-------------------|
| 0 client projects | ~50% of market rate |
| 3 completed projects | ~80% of market rate |
| 10 projects + testimonials | 100–120% of market rate |
| 20+ projects + retainer clients | 150–200% of market rate |

Every finished project raises what you can charge next: portfolio grows, testimonials accumulate, confidence increases — prices follow.

## Network = net worth
Every client knows other business owners; every great delivery is a referral waiting to happen. Treat every project — even routine ones — as your audition for the next opportunity.

## Rules
- Spec samples use the prospect's real branding — never generic.
- Ship samples *before* pitching; show, don't ask.
- Raise price at each tier, not arbitrarily mid-relationship.
