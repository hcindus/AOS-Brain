# SOP-001 — Lead Response & Qualification

**KPI:** 5-minute first response · 40%+ conversion to qualified · tracked in the lead portal.

## Step 1 — Acknowledge (0–5 min)
Make contact while the lead is hot. If a phone number exists, text immediately:
> "Hi [First Name], this is [Agent] from Performance Supply Depot. I got your inquiry about [product]. I'm pulling options together now — can you chat 2 minutes?"

Check **all** sources every 15 min (8am–6pm): form, missed call/voicemail, email, event scans, referrals.

## Step 2 — Qualify (BANT-lite)
- **Budget** — can they afford it?
- **Authority** — are they the decision-maker?
- **Need** — what problem are they solving?
- **Timeline** — when do they need it?

## Step 3 — Score
| Score | Criteria | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 HOT | Ready to buy, budget + authority + near-term | Quote within 2h (SOP-002) |
| 🟡 WARM | Interested, but some friction | Nurture + follow-up in 24h |
| ❄️ COLD | Vague / not a fit | Light-touch, revisit in 30 days |

## Step 4 — Ghosted-lead recovery
If no reply after 48h, send the recovery sequence:
1. Day 3: "Wanted to make sure I didn't miss anything — still need [product]?"
2. Day 7: "I'll close your file unless I hear otherwise. One quick call?"
3. Day 14: "Closing your file now — reach out anytime if it comes back up."

## KPI check
Did I respond within 5 min? Did I score + log it? Am I tracking conversion toward 40%+?
