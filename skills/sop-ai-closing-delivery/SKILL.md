# SOP-047 — AI Closing & Delivery

**KPI:** 90%+ onboarding completion · first win within 48 hours of signup.

## Phase 1 — The Close
1. **Soft close**: "Does this feel like the right solution for [Company]?"
2. **Handle final objection** (see `sop-ai-objection-handling`).
3. **Confirm** — agreement, payment terms (50% deposit), start date.

## Phase 2 — Delivery / Onboarding
1. **Kickoff** — within 24h of signup.
2. **Setup** — deliver the product/implementation, walkthrough.
3. **First win** — get them a tangible result within 48h (build the "wow" moment).
4. **Handoff** — document, set expectations, schedule check-ins.

## Rule
Never leave a new client in limbo — the first 48 hours decide retention.
