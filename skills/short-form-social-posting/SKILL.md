# Short-Form Social Posting (TikTok + Instagram)

**KPI:** Maximize algorithmic reach for a new creator by nailing the first frame, caption, hashtags, and first-hour engagement.

## Same file, different captioning
The same 9:16 vertical trailer works on both platforms — the difference is how you caption it and how you engage after posting.

## TikTok
- **First frame is everything.** A visually strong opening gets pushed; black/slow dies before anyone sees it. Lead with your best shot.
- Turn on **auto-captions** if the film has dialogue.
- 3–5 hashtags: `#AIFilm #AIVideo #7ART #CinematicAI #AIFilmmaking`
- Post at peak hours for your timezone — typically 7–9 PM.
- **Respond to the first 10 comments within the first hour** — signals the algorithm it's worth distributing.

## Instagram Reels
- Caption matters more than on TikTok; Instagram rewards content that keeps people on-platform — a slightly longer caption helps.
- Write 2–4 sentences, not a wall of text.
- Choose the **cover image manually** (a striking frame, not Instagram's auto-select).
- Share to **main feed AND Stories** for double visibility.
- Post and stay — first-hour engagement matters most. Post when you can respond, not right before bed.

## Rules
- Opening frame must be striking on TikTok — never lead with black/slow.
- Hashtags: 3–5, specific + broad mix.
- First hour = engagement window: comment replies on TikTok, staying online on IG.
