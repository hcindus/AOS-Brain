# Cross-Platform Publish Order

**KPI:** One canonical destination (the full-film YouTube link) reachable from every platform, in ~15 minutes.

## Sequence (in order)
1. **YouTube first** — so you have a link to share everywhere else.
2. Then TikTok, then Instagram (and any other platforms).
3. **Final key move:** paste the full-film YouTube link into the description *or first comment* on every other platform.

## Why
Creates a single destination where people can watch the full film, regardless of where they first discovered you — turning short-form reach into full-film views.

## Rules
- YouTube always first; everything downstream needs its link.
- The link goes on EVERY other platform — description or first comment.
