# SOP-044 — AI Qualifying

**KPI:** 95% reduction in unqualified calls · 80%+ of calendar filled with qualified buyers.

## Process
1. **First contact** — AI agent reaches out; permission check: "Do you have 2 minutes about your supply needs?"
2. **Qualify** — BANT: budget, authority, need, timeline.
3. **Route** — qualified → book calendar; unqualified → nurture/archive.
4. **Handoff** — pass a structured summary to the rep before the call.

## Rules
- Never book a human call without qualification.
- Log every outcome (qualified / not / reschedule).
