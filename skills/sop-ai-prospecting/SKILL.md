# SOP-043 — AI Prospecting

**KPI:** 10,000+ qualified prospects / quarter · 80%+ ICP match accuracy.

## Framework — 10-80-10
- **10%** human ideation (define ICP, angle)
- **80%** AI execution (scrape, enrich, score)
- **10%** human integration (review + load to CRM)

## Process
1. **ICP definition** (60 min) — firmographics, top 20% of customers, pain points.
2. **AI sourcing** — scrape/enrich prospects matching ICP (name, contact, company, size).
3. **Scoring** — rank by ICP fit; discard below threshold.
4. **Load** — push qualified prospects into the lead portal / CRM for outreach.

## KPI check
Am I hitting 10k/quarter? Is match accuracy ≥ 80%? Are low-fit leads being filtered?
