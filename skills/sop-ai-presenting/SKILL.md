# SOP-045 — AI Presenting

**KPI:** custom proposal < 10 minutes · 35%+ close rate on presented offers.

## Process
1. **Gather** — pull lead context, needs, product fit, pricing (5 min).
2. **Generate** — build the proposal (use `jarvis_core` `QuoteEngine` for quotes / proposal doc).
3. **Customize** — tailor to the specific pain points + likely objections (3–5 min).
4. **Deliver** — send + schedule the follow-up call (2 min).

## Contents of a strong proposal
- Problem → solution → proof → investment → clear next step
- Likely objections + responses baked in
