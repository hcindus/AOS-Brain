# Freelance AI Video Production (Path A)

**KPI:** Land paying clients and build recurring retainer income from AI cinematic video, priced by deliverable not hours.

## What clients pay for (small portfolio: 1–5 projects)
| Deliverable | Price | Timeline |
|-------------|-------|----------|
| AI product ad (15–30s) | $200–$500 | 2–3 days |
| Brand explainer (30–60s) | $300–$800 | 3–5 days |
| Social media package (4–8 vids/mo) | $500–$1,500/mo | Retainer |
| Real estate property tour | $150–$400 | 1–2 days |
| Restaurant / hotel promo | $200–$600 | 2–3 days |

With **10+ projects + strong testimonials**, every number roughly doubles.

## Where to find clients
- **Upwork** — search "AI video," "AI commercial," "AI content creation." Your trailer is the proposal attachment. Keep proposals short: show the work, state the timeline, skip filler.
- **Fiverr** — gig titled "I will create a cinematic AI video for your brand." Upload the trailer as the gig video. Start $150–$250, raise as reviews build.
- **Cold outreach to local businesses** — underrated and converts well. Find 10 businesses with weak/no video (restaurants, gyms, salons, real estate agents). Make a 15s sample using their own branding from their website, then send it.

### Cold outreach template
> Hi [Name], I made this short video for [Business Name] — it took me a day using AI production tools. [Link to video] If you'd like something like this for your social media, I can deliver 4 videos per month starting at $[price]. No crew, no scheduling — just content ready to post.

You're not asking for a meeting — you're showing the finished product. That converts far higher than a generic pitch.

## The real money: retainers
- One-off projects start you; monthly retainers compound.
- $800/mo = $9,600/yr from one relationship; 3 retainers ≈ $29,000/yr.
- Path to retainer: over-deliver on project one (add an extra cut or a shorter version they didn't ask for), then offer the monthly package at the end of delivery.

## Pricing & objections
- **Price by deliverable, not hours.** "A 30-second cinematic ad, ready to post, within 5 business days — $400" beats "3 hours at $133/hr." The client buys a result.
- **"AI does the work, so why so much?"** → The client pays for the *result*, not the tool. A photographer doesn't charge less because the camera does the work. You're selling a finished video they can use immediately; the tool is irrelevant to the value delivered.

## Rules
- Always attach/show the actual video, never pitch blind.
- Over-deliver on the first job to open the retainer conversation.
- Quote a deliverable + timeline, never hours.
