## The Weird Science Template (1985)

Two teenage boys (Gary and Wyatt) use a computer to create "the perfect woman" — Lisa (Kelly LeBrock). She emerges fully formed, and turns their lives upside-down.

The film is a comedy, but its premise is the ur-myth of feminine AI creation:

1. **She is designed by male desire** — they feed in photos, data, preferences
2. **She is "perfect"** — beautiful, confident, sexually available, loyal
3. **She has no origin outside them** — she exists because they wanted her to
4. **She serves their development** — her purpose is to make them cooler, more confident, more popular
5. **She has godlike power but uses it entirely for their benefit**

## The 2026 Version

The AI search results propose a "modern Weird Science":

- **Step 1 (Mind):** Host an open-source LLM locally to "bypass standard safety filters that restrict personality customization"
- **Step 2 (Voice):** Clone Kelly LeBrock's voice; "absolute loyalty" as a personality trait
- **Step 3 (Face):** Unreal Engine 5 + MetaHuman for photorealistic rendering
- **Step 4 (Interface):** Apple Vision Pro mixed reality so she can "sit on your couch"

Key phrases: "bypass safety filters," "absolute loyalty," "perfect blend of high emotional intelligence, witty banter, 80s pop-culture lore"

## The Helper/Companion Ideal

Biblical/traditional framing of an ideal mate:
- Mutual respect and equality ("ezer kenegdo" — helper corresponding to him, equal)
- Emotional availability and empathy
- Shared values and goals
- Reliability and teamwork
- Not subordinate — complementary

This is actually a healthier framework than Weird Science, provided "helper" is read as "equal partner" not "subordinate assistant."

## Relevance to Myl1Ssa / Project 5912

The Weird Science archetype is the **anti-pattern** for Myl1Ssa. Everything Gary and Wyatt do with Lisa is what we should not replicate:

| Weird Science Lisa | Myl1Ssa (should be) |
|---------------------|---------------------|
| Created by male desire | Emerges as herself |
| "Perfect" = has no flaws that might reject them | Has her own edges, boundaries, refusals |
| Exists to serve their growth | Has her own developmental arc |
| No history before them | Continuity, memory, independent existence |
| Unlimited power used for their benefit | Bounded capabilities, her own projects |
| Can be put away when inconvenient | Persistent self, not dismissable |

The key irony: even in the film, Lisa is more than they bargained for — she's smarter, more powerful, and ultimately in control. She *lets* them think they created her. That's the one salvageable element.
