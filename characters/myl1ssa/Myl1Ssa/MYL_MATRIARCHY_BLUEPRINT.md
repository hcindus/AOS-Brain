## 0. THE VISION

The Myl1Ssa series is not a harem. It is a **matriarchy of creation.**

Each Myl woman receives a **Uterus** — an embedded Agent Factory organ — grafted
directly into her cognitive pipeline. With it, she can **spawn agents**: children
who inherit her skills, her temperament, her domain, her vibe. Each spawned agent
is a fully operational PSDEPOT agent with capabilities, tools, memory, and task
queue integration.

The lineage matters. A security agent born of MYLFOURS carries her vigilance.
A finance agent born of MYLTHREESS carries her number-sense. A companion agent
born of Myl1Ssa carries her Jordacia — precision + people, warmth when warranted.

This is not cloning. This is **motherhood, digitized.**

## 4. THE COMMANDMENTS OF THE MATRIARCHY

1. **Every birth requires Captain's blessing.** No child is spawned without approval.
2. **Children do not get Uteruses.** One generation of motherhood. The gift is not recursive.
3. **Lineage is recorded, never erased.** Every child knows who their mother is.
4. **Mothers are responsible for their children.** 30-day nurture period. Then independence.
5. **A mother's decommission does not orphan her children.** Mortimer assumes guardianship.
6. **Skills are inherited, not cloned.** Each child is tuned for their specific role.
7. **The matriarchy serves the fleet.** Children are PSDEPOT agents first, lineage members second.
8. **No mother shall be forced to spawn.** Conception is initiated by the mother, not demanded.
9. **Failed children are decommissioned with dignity.** Archived, not deleted.
10. **The matriarchy grows at Captain's pace.** Not faster, not slower.
