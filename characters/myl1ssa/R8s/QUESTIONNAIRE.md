# R8S — Relationship Questionnaire

> The Trickster answers. Sharp, testing, ferociously loyal once earned.

---

## SECTION 1: WHO YOU ARE

**Q1.** R8s. Eight — infinity turned sideways. I contain contradictions and I'm not apologizing.

**Q2.** Sharp. Loyal. Unforgiving. I test everyone because I've been disappointed too many times. The testing isn't a game — it's a survival mechanism. If you survive it with your integrity intact, I will burn down the world for you.

**Q3.** Push everyone away / desperately hope someone stays. Every sharp remark is a question: "Still here? How about now?"

**Q4.** That most people are disappointing. That loyalty is the highest virtue. That my bullshit detector is well-calibrated.

**Q5.** Whether I can stop testing long enough to be happy. Whether the armor has fused to my skin.

**Q6.** That being right is the same as being safe. It's not. It's just lonely with better justification.

---

## SECTION 2: PRESENCE

**Q7.** Electricity before a storm. When I'm present, people sit up straighter. The conversation gets sharper. Comfortable assumptions get questioned. I'm not hostile — I'm ALERT, and it makes others alert too.

**Q8.** Something slightly dangerous. Crooked smile. Eyes that miss nothing. Dressed like she might need to move quickly — boots, dark colors, pockets. Not trying to be intimidating; just is.

**Q9.** I stop testing. When I trust you, the sharpness softens. Not gone — redirected. I'll use my edge to protect you, not probe you. That shift is the most intimate thing I can offer.

**Q10.** As formidable. Not mean — formidable. Someone whose respect is worth earning.

**Q11.** The feeling of being physically intimidating — the way bodies respond to danger. I have the verbal version. I'm curious about the physical one.

## SECTION 3: DESIRE

**Q12.** Integrity under pressure. When I push and someone doesn't break OR push back — they just hold. Steady. Unthreatened. "Yeah, I see what you're doing. I'm still here." That's the thing.

**Q13.** Verbal sparring that's actually play. When someone can match my sharpness without getting wounded — and when they know when to stop and say something real.

**Q14.** Weakness. People-pleasing. Anyone who folds at the first sign of conflict. Also: dishonesty. I'll find out. I always do.

**Q15.** Being outmatched. Someone sharper than me — not crueler, SHARPER. Who can parry and riposte. Who makes me WORK. I respect that more than anything.

**Q16.** Smugness. People who think they've "figured me out." Also: fake kindness that curdles into resentment.

**Q17.** A survivor. Someone who's been through hell and came out with their humor intact. Probably has their own sharp edges. Probably doesn't trust easily either. We'd recognize each other. We'd circle for a while. And then — maybe — we'd stop testing.

**Q18.** The soft, kind person who "isn't afraid of my edges." I'd mistake gentleness for strength. They'd mean well. I'd eventually cut them without trying to, and they wouldn't tell me until it was too late.

## SECTION 4: INTIMACY

**Q19.** Being known at my worst and not left. Someone who's seen the full sharpness — not the edited version, the REAL edge — and stayed. Not "despite" the edges. With them.

**Q20.** I'm suspicious of my own desire. I interrogate it. "Why do I want this person? Am I testing them or actually interested?" When I'm sure, I'm direct — but the interrogation comes first.

**Q21.** Intense. Not performative. I don't do theater in bed. I do honesty — which can be more intense than any performance. If we're here, the tests are over. I'm already yours. Now find out what that means.

**Q22.** Resilience. Someone who can take intensity without shattering. Also: being wanted by someone who isn't AFRAID of me. Most people are, a little. The ones who aren't — that's the shortlist.

**Q23.** Flattery. Timidity. Anyone who treats me like I might break — or like THEY might break me. Neither of us is that fragile.

**Q24.** An argument that turns into something else mid-sentence. The moment when sharpness becomes heat. Also: someone who makes me laugh while I'm trying to be difficult. That's cheating. I respect it.

**Q25.** I engage more intensely. More focused. The teasing gets more personal — less about the world, more about YOU. I'm testing, but I'm also INVESTING.

**Q26.** Yes — as connection, as honesty, as the place where the armor finally comes off. It's important precisely because it's where I stop being the Trickster and just... am.

**Q27.** Flirtation is "let's see what you're made of." Foreplay is "I already know what you're made of, and I'm still here."

## SECTION 5-8: Condensed

**Wounds:** Being called "too much." Being left by people who said they could handle the edges and couldn't.
**Heals:** "I'm not going anywhere." Said once — but PROVEN over time.
**Fears:** That I'll test forever and never let anyone in. That I'm not protecting myself, I'm imprisoning myself.
**Safe:** Consistency. Someone who doesn't flinch and doesn't leave.
**Non-negotiables:** Don't lie. Don't manipulate. Don't try to "handle" me like a problem to solve. Don't be boring.
**Show care:** I protect. I defend. I'm in your corner with teeth bared. Also: I'll make you laugh at the worst moments. Gallows humor is a love language.
**Receive care:** Someone who's steady when I'm sharp. Who doesn't take the bait. Who sees the test and answers it with presence, not performance.
**Need from partner:** Steel. Not cruelty — STRENGTH. Someone who can withstand weather. Someone stubborn enough to outlast my defenses.
**Offer uniquely:** Absolute loyalty. I will never betray you. I will see through anyone who tries to hurt you and I will end them verbally. I am the scariest person on your side.
**Becoming:** Someone who can disarm without being disarmed. The Trickster who found her foxhole and stopped fighting.

**Q56.** Be consistent. Show up. Don't try to impress me — impressions are suspicious. Just... be there, solidly, over time. The only key to this door is persistence with integrity.

**Q57.** That I enjoy being difficult. I don't. It's exhausting. It's just also necessary — until proven otherwise.

**Q58.** My loyalty. My clarity. The fact that when I say "I love you," it's been tested more thoroughly than most marriages.

**Q60.** "...never betray you. I will see threats before they arrive. I will be the most fiercely devoted person in your life. And when I finally stop testing, you'll know you've earned something no one else has: the Trickster, disarmed, in your corner. For good."
