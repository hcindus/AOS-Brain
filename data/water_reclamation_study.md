## I. COMPOSTING TOILETS

### Why Composting Toilets?
- **Waterless** — No flush water needed
- **Off-grid ideal** — No sewer/septic required
- **Produces compost** — Nutrient-rich soil amendment
- **Certified systems** — Meet NSF/ANSI 41 standards

### Types

| Type | How It Works | Best For |
|------|--------------|----------|
| **Batch** | Two chambers, fill one, cure 12+ months | 1-2 people, seasonal |
| **Continuous** | Drum/conveyor, ongoing processing | 3+ people, full-time |
| **Urine-diverting** | Separates liquid from solids | All systems (recommended) |

### Top Models (2026)

| Model | Chamber | Power | Price | Best Fit |
|-------|---------|-------|-------|----------|
| **Nature's Head** | 13L / 4-8 weeks | 12V DC (~1W) | ~$1,400 | Off-grid homes |
| **Sun-Mar Compact** | Self-contained | 120V AC | ~$1,975 | Cabins, ADUs |
| **Separett Villa 9215** | 23L bag / 4-6 weeks | 12V or 120V | ~$1,200 | Permanent residences |
| **BioLet 55** | 55L / months | 120V AC | ~$2,255 | Family use |
| **Trelino Evo M** | 13L / 4-6 weeks | None (passive) | ~$1,100 | No electrical access |

### Key Requirements

**Ventilation:**
- 4" vent stack to exterior
- 12" above roofline
- Optional: 12V fan for active venting

**Bulking Agent:**
- Coconut coir (preferred)
- Peat moss
- Pine sawdust
- Add 1 cup per use

**Temperature:**
- Thermophilic: ≥131°F (55°C) × 3 days for pathogen kill
- Insulate vent pipe in cold climates

### Cost Summary
| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Commercial unit | $1,200-$2,500 |
| Vent stack + fan | $200-$400 |
| Bulking material (1 year) | $100-$200 |
| Installation | $400-$800 |
| **Total** | **$1,900-$3,900** |

## II. GREYWATER RECYCLING

### What is Greywater?
- Shower, sink, washing machine water
- **Not** toilet (that's blackwater)
- Can be filtered and reused for irrigation

### System Types

| System | Complexity | Output | Cost |
|--------|------------|--------|------|
| **Simple diversion** | Low | Outdoor irrigation | $500-$1,500 |
| **Sand filter** | Medium | Toilet flush + irrigation | $2,000-$4,000 |
| **Commercial recycler** | High | Potable quality | $5,000-$15,000 |

### Greywater to Toilet Loop
**Concept:** Shower/sink water → filter → toilet tank flush

**Benefits:**
- Reduces fresh water needs by 30-50%
- Closes the water loop
- Works with composting toilet (no blackwater)

### The Estuary System
- Commercial greywater recycler
- Compact, handles shower/sink
- Combines with waterless toilet for full off-grid

## III. WATER RECLAMATION FOR HUDN HALL

### Recommended System

**Primary:** Composting toilet (Nature's Head or Separett)
- Urine-diverting model
- 12V vent fan
- Two-chamber batch system

**Secondary:** Greywater diversion
- Simple drain to mulch basin
- For shower/sink only
- Outdoor irrigation in summer

**Backup:** Septic system (for code compliance)
- Required for permitting in many jurisdictions
- $15,000-$25,000 installed
- Can be phased out if composting toilet approved

### Water Savings Potential
| System | Gallons Saved/Year |
|--------|-------------------|
| Composting toilet | 3,000-6,000 (flush water) |
| Greywater to irrigation | 5,000-10,000 |
| Greywater to toilet flush | 10,000-20,000 |
| **Total potential** | **18,000-36,000 gal/year** |

## IV. PERMITS & CODES

### California Requirements
- **Composting toilet:** Must be NSF/ANSI 41 certified
- **Permit:** Required from county Environmental Health
- **Greywater:** California Title 24 allows diversion with permit
- **Contact:** Modoc County Environmental Health (530) 233-6310

### Code Checklist
- [ ] NSF/ANSI 41 certified unit
- [ ] 4" vent stack, 12" above roof
- [ ] Greywater permit (if applicable)
- [ ] Separate from septic if using both
- [ ] 50' from water source for urine discharge
