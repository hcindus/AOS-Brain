## II. WATER SYSTEMS AT HUDN HALL

### Current Plan (from Architectural Brief)

**Water Shed (16' × 16')**
- Roof collection: 200 sq ft
- Annual yield: ~1,200 gal/year
- Storage: Two 350-gallon tanks (700 gal total)
- Power: Solar panel + battery + pump

### Supplemental: Half-Moon Ditch System
**Source:** Traditional method, Senegal Sahel (Great Green Wall)

**Method:**
- Shallow elongated trench
- Straight side for water inflow
- Curved embankment side to hold water
- Slows flow → soil infiltration → groundwater recharge

**Benefits:**
- Low cost, minimal tech
- Complements Water Shed
- Groundwater recharge

## III. MODOC COUNTY WATER REALITIES

### Climate
- ~12 inches rain/year
- Cold winters, warm summers
- Snowmelt = spring water source

### Water Sources
| Source | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| **Rainwater** | Free, simple | Seasonal, limited |
| **Well** | Year-round supply | $50,000+, 3-6 months |
| **Snowmelt** | Spring surge | Seasonal |
| **Groundwater (ditches)** | Recharge wells | Slow, site-dependent |

### Recommendation
1. **Primary:** Well (required for permanent residence)
2. **Supplement:** Rainwater collection (Water Shed)
3. **Backup:** Half-moon ditches for groundwater recharge

## IV. SYSTEM SPECIFICATIONS

### Rainwater Collection Formula
```
Annual Yield (gallons) = Roof Area (sq ft) × Annual Rainfall (ft) × 7.48 gal/cu ft × 0.85 (efficiency)
```

**For 200 sq ft @ 12" rain/year:**
```
200 × 1.0 × 7.48 × 0.85 = 1,272 gal/year
```

### Storage Sizing
- **Dry season reserve:** 3-6 months (300-600 gal minimum)
- **Recommended:** 1,000+ gallon storage for reliability

### Filtration
| Stage | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| Debris screen | Leaves, twigs |
| First-flush diverter | Dirty initial rain |
| Sediment filter | Particles |
| UV or chlorine | Pathogen kill |
| Activated carbon | Taste/odor |
