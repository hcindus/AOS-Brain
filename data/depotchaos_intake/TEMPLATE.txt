DepotChaos Intake — paste format
================================

Drop a record here and it auto-syncs to the CRM (unified.db leads table).
Two ways to write it:

1) JSON (best — drop as a .json file in inbox/)

{
  "business_name": "Bellam Self Storage",
  "phone": "415-454-1983",
  "contact_name": "Andree",
  "address": "24 Bellam Blvd",
  "city": "San Rafael",
  "state": "CA",
  "zip": "94901",
  "amount": 1179.81,
  "invoice": "3106102",
  "status": "contacted",
  "callback_date": "2026-11-15"
}

2) Pipe-delimited line (one record per line, .txt file)
Fields in order (leave empty between pipes to skip):

business_name | email | phone | phone_alt | contact_name | contact_title | website | address | city | state | zip | amount | invoice | status | callback_date | notes

Example:
Bellam Self Storage ||415-454-1983||Andree|||24 Bellam Blvd|San Rafael|CA|94901|1179.81|3106102|contacted|2026-11-15|

Rules:
- business_name is required (everything else optional).
- Existing records are UPDATED (only non-empty fields overwrite); new ones are INSERTED.
- amount + invoice auto-formatted into the notes field.
- status "contacted" sets last_contact = today; callback_date sets next_contact.

The processor runs every 15 min and archives processed files to processed/.
