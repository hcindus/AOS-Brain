## I. MODERN YURT SYSTEMS

### Steel-Frame Yurts (WeatherPort Style)
**Best for:** Extreme climates, commercial use, high wind/snow loads

| Component | Material | Notes |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| Frame | High-strength steel | Not wood lattice |
| Cover | Tensioned fabric membrane | UV-resistant, mildew-resistant |
| Insulation | EnerLayer™ radiant barrier | Achieves any R-value |
| Snow load | Up to 80 PSF | Engineered per location |
| Wind load | Up to 120 MPH | Site-specific engineering |

**Performance Data (650 sq ft example):**
- 35% less heat required at -25°F (vs. uninsulated)
- 22% less cooling at +125°F ambient
- 33% AC reduction with solar fly

---

### Wood-Frame Yurts (Pacific Yurt Style)
**Best for:** Residential, aesthetic, standard climates

| Component | Material | Notes |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| Lattice wall | Douglas fir lath | Flexible, carries load |
| Tension cable | Steel | Around top of lattice |
| Rafters | Douglas fir | Notch onto cable |
| Center ring | Laminated compression | Spreads load evenly |
| Snow/wind kit | Vertical rafter supports | Direct load path |

**Key Innovation:** Tension-compression system
- Lattice carries compression
- Cable carries tension
- Rafters transfer load to ring
- Result: Unified structural system

## II. MODERN MATERIALS

### Insulation Options

| Type | R-Value | Best For |
|------|---------|----------|
| **Reflective foil** | Varies | Summer heat rejection |
| **EnerLayer™** | Custom | Extreme climates |
| **Fiberglass blanket** | R-13 to R-30 | Cold climates |
| **Spray foam** | R-6/inch | High efficiency |
| **Wool/green** | R-3.5/inch | Natural option |

**Pro Tip:** Radiant barrier > standard insulation for yurts
- Reflects interior heat back in
- Reflects exterior heat out
- Works with convection insulation

### Fabrics

| Cover Type | Life Span | Notes |
|------------|-----------|-------|
| Standard canvas | 5-10 years | Basic protection |
| Architectural membrane | 15-25 years | UV/mildew resistant |
| Premium fabric | 20-30 years | Extra UV treatment |

**Requirements:**
- Flame resistance (ASTM E-108)
- UV resistance
- Mildew resistance
- Tear strength

## III. CLIMATE ADAPTATION

### Cold Climate (Modoc County, CA)
**Needs:** Heat retention, snow load, moisture management

**Recommendations:**
- [ ] R-30+ insulation (walls + roof)
- [ ] Radiant barrier under roof
- [ ] Snow/wind kit (80 PSF)
- [ ] Thermal windows (dual-pane)
- [ ] Rocket mass heater or wood stove
- [ ] Vapor barrier on interior

**Design:** South-facing windows for solar gain, thermal mass inside

### Hot Climate
**Needs:** Heat rejection, ventilation, UV protection

**Recommendations:**
- [ ] Reflective exterior color (white/beige)
- [ ] Radiant barrier insulation
- [ ] Venting dome (42" optional)
- [ ] Operable windows on opposite walls
- [ ] Solar fly (shade over top)
- [ ] Ceiling fan for air circulation

**Design:** Cross ventilation, overhangs for shade

## IV. YURT PLATFORM DESIGN

### Required Elements
1. **Foundation:** Elevated platform (16"-24" above grade)
2. **Floor framing:** Treated lumber, joists
3. **Decking:** Plywood or composite
4. **Anchoring:** Cable ties or concrete

### Platform Specifications (20' yurt)
```
Platform: 20' diameter (314 sq ft)
Height: 16"-24" (above grade)
Joists: 16" OC, 2x8 or 2x10
Decking: 3/4" plywood or composite
Rail: Treated 2x6 or 4x4
```

## VI. DESIGN DETAILS

### Window Placement (Climate-Based)
| Climate | Placement | Purpose |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| Cold | South wall | Solar gain |
| Hot | North, shaded | Cool air intake |
| Any | Opposite walls | Cross ventilation |

### Ventilation System
1. **Venting dome:** Top center, hot air exhaust
2. **Windows:** Lower wall, cool air intake
3. **Door:** Opposite dome, airflow path
4. **Result:** Natural convection cooling
