# Highway 116 — Restaurants, Cafes & Burger Joints
### Jenner → Guerneville → Forestville → Graton → Sebastopol → Cotati → Rohnert Park → Petaluma

**Compiled:** Oct 2, 2026 · Source: OpenStreetMap (Overpass) + restaurant websites
**Route:** CA-116 runs west→east from the coast (Jenner) along the Russian River, through Sebastopol, then south/east through Cotati to Petaluma (US-101). Rohnert Park is adjacent (on 101, bordering Cotati).
**Note:** Emails are rare for restaurants — website is the contact channel. `—` = no public number found. Confirm hours before visiting.

## Route Summary (west → east)
1. **Jenner** — 3 (coastal seafood, coffee)
2. **Guerneville** — ~20 (Russian River casual + farm-to-table)
3. **Forestville** — 6 (Italian, pizza, drive-in)
4. **Graton** — 4 (Willow Wood, Underwood — both destination spots)
5. **Sebastopol** — ~55 (The Barlow food hall + Gravenstein Hwy corridor)
6. **Cotati / Rohnert Park** — ~50 (La Plaza + 101 commerce strip)
7. **Petaluma** — ~45 (downtown + McDowell Blvd)

*All data from OpenStreetMap (Overpass) + restaurant websites. Hours change — confirm before visiting.*
