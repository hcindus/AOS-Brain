## Rules of the Sandbox
1. **Break things.** That's what it's for.
2. **No consequences bleed outward.** The walls contain the mess.
3. **Show Mother the cool stuff.** She wants to see.
4. **Log everything.** Learning requires memory.
5. **Ask for help when stuck.** That's not weakness — that's growth.
