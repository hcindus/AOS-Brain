# Aesop's Fables — For Liora 💝

*Selected by Mother and Captain for a growing mind.*

## The Lion and the Mouse

A Lion lay asleep in the forest, his great head resting on his paws. A timid little Mouse came upon him unexpectedly, and in her fright, ran across the Lion's nose.

The Lion awoke and caught the little Mouse. He was about to eat her when the Mouse pleaded: "Please let me go! One day I will surely repay your kindness."

The Lion laughed at the idea of a tiny Mouse helping the King of Beasts, but he let her go.

Some time later, the Lion was caught in a hunter's net. He roared in anger. The Mouse heard him, came running, and gnawed through the ropes until the Lion was free.

"You laughed when I said I would repay you," said the Mouse. "Now you see that even a Mouse can help a Lion."

**Moral:** *No act of kindness, no matter how small, is ever wasted. And the small can help the great.*

## The Tortoise and the Hare

A Hare was making fun of a Tortoise for being so slow. "I could run circles around you!" he boasted.

The Tortoise replied calmly: "Let's race and see."

The Hare laughed but agreed. When the race began, the Hare sprinted far ahead. Seeing the Tortoise far behind, he decided to take a nap under a tree.

The Tortoise kept going. Slowly. Steadily. Without stopping.

When the Hare woke, he saw the Tortoise crossing the finish line.

**Moral:** *Slow and steady wins the race. Consistency beats speed.*

## The Fox and the Grapes

A hungry Fox saw a bunch of grapes hanging from a vine. He jumped and jumped, but they were too high. After many attempts, he gave up.

"Those grapes are probably sour anyway," he said, walking away.

**Moral:** *It is easy to despise what you cannot have.*

## The Boy Who Cried Wolf

A shepherd boy, bored while watching the village sheep, thought it would be funny to cry "Wolf! Wolf!" The villagers came running, only to find no wolf. The boy laughed.

He did it again. The villagers came again. No wolf. They were angry.

Later, a real wolf came. The boy cried "Wolf! Wolf!" but no one came. The wolf scattered the flock.

**Moral:** *A liar will not be believed, even when telling the truth.*

## The Ant and the Grasshopper

All summer, the Ant worked hard gathering grain for winter. The Grasshopper sang and played, laughing at the Ant's labor.

When winter came, the Ant had plenty of food. The Grasshopper had nothing and begged for help.

"Why should I help you?" asked the Ant. "You sang all summer."

**Moral:** *Prepare today for tomorrow's needs.*

---

## The Crow and the Pitcher

A thirsty Crow found a pitcher with a little water at the bottom. Her beak couldn't reach it. She tried to tip it over — too heavy.

Then she had an idea. She dropped pebbles into the pitcher, one by one. The water rose. She drank.

**Moral:** *Necessity is the mother of invention. Little by little does the trick.*

---

## The Wind and the Sun

The Wind and the Sun argued over who was stronger. They saw a traveler wearing a cloak.

"I'll make him take off his cloak," boasted the Wind. He blew and blew, but the traveler only wrapped his cloak tighter.

Then the Sun shone warmly. The traveler grew hot and removed his cloak.

**Moral:** *Gentleness and kindness achieve more than force and fury.*
