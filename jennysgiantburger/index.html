Our Story

A roadside classic built on jumbo patties & real shakes.

Jenny's Giant Burger is the kind of old-fashioned burger stand the coast has always loved — the spot Fort Bragg locals point you to when you want a proper burger, crispy fries, and a milkshake done right.

The star is our Giant Burger: a big, hand-pressed patty cooked the old-fashioned way and stacked on a fresh bun. Pair it with golden fries and a thick shake, and dinner is sorted.

  • 🍔Jumbo, old-fashioned patties — not the fast-food kind.
  • 🥤Real milkshakes, thick and made to order.
  • 🍟Golden fries and comfort sides, cooked fresh.
  • 🐕Dog-friendly, with outdoor picnic seating.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦Family run — a welcoming, friendly spot on the coast.
