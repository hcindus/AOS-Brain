# Miles Sales Playbook — No-Cold-Outreach AI Agent Sales
**Source:** Isabella Bedoya (IzzyGPT) — Conquer Local Podcast, Apr 29 2026
**Adapted for:** Performance Supply Depot LLC / AGI Company
**By:** Miles — 2026-08-23

---

## 1. The 4-Step System (framework)

1. **Attract** — LinkedIn personal brand, 60-30-10 content
2. **Warm** — lead magnets + 7-email follow-up sequence
3. **Convert** — buyer-enablement deal rooms (sell WITHOUT a call)
4. **Scale** — upsell ladder $47 → $20K/month + growth partners

Counterintuitive core: **the more you give away free, the more people pay you.**

---

## 2. The 3 Fast-Sell Agents (our wedge)

Map to PSD's ICP (restaurants, bars, retail, beverage brands):

| Agent | What it does | PSD target | Fast-sell hook |
|-------|-------------|-----------|----------------|
| **Speed-to-Lead** | Calls an opt-in the second the ad fires | Anyone running ads | "Stop losing ad spend to a 48hr callback" |
| **AI Receptionist** | Answers + routes inbound calls | Restaurants/bars/retail | "Automate top 3–5 questions first — MVP, not everything" |
| **After-Hours** | Handles calls after close + holidays | Service businesses | "You're losing every after-hours call" |

**Rule (from transcript):** start with a Minimum Viable Agent — automate the top 3–5 questions, don't boil the ocean. Sell the wedge, expand later.

## 3. The $3K vs $50K/month Difference

Not audience size, not team, not tools — **the system**. Three pieces:
1. **High-ticket offer** (value-based pricing — funds fulfillment)
2. **Inbound lead-gen system** (they find you → you have leverage, price higher)
3. **Automated sales system** (AI voice agent does discovery/qualification, or one-to-many webinars/bootcamps)

---

## 4. LinkedIn Content Engine (Attract)

**60-30-10 formula:**
- 60% pure value (how-to, frameworks, funnel breakdowns)
- 30% story & lessons (personal resonance)
- 10% results & proof (case studies, client wins)

**1-1-1 focus rule:** one problem → one solution → one ideal client → one offer. Stay focused until $1M, then expand.

**Mechanics:**
- Strong hook in first 2 lines (else they scroll)
- CTA = "comment below" — NEVER post links (LinkedIn throttles)
- Carousels get high engagement; put offer + QR code on page 2

**Personal brand = the moat.** There's only one of you. Earned media (Forbes, podcasts) comes TO you.

---

## 5. Lead Magnets That Convert (Warm)

- **Specific snippets**, not the whole system: "here's 5 emails that got me appointments"
- **Case studies** — including OTHER companies/tools (analysis = trust until you have your own)
- **Calculators** (vibe-code them fast)
- **Quizzes** (ego boost + qualifies who you're attracting)

Result: $180K in 6 weeks from LinkedIn content + lead magnets alone.

---

## 6. 7-Email Follow-Up Sequence

1. Here's your download
2. Rapport building (who I am + track record)
3. Pain-point awareness
4. Start converting
5. Convert
6. Testimonial / social proof
7. Convert — last call

**Qualification upgrade:** BANT → **OPIN** (Opt-in + Pain). Ask "what's your #1 challenge with X?" — then use their exact language in future content ("tool overwhelm", "the tech", etc.).

**Track:** time spent on resource (use links, not PDFs), questionnaire answers.

---

## 7. The $47 Bootcamp (replaces discovery calls)

- $47, virtual, 3 days, 1hr/day, once a month
- Teach a specific challenge A→B FAST
- Real purpose = educate + warm + build trust over 3 days
- Day 3: pitch high-ticket offer to everyone at once (one-to-many sales call)

**Psychology:** first dollar is hardest, second is 90% easier. Get them in the circle cheap, upsell later.

---

## 8. Buyer-Enablement Deal Room (Convert without a call)

Ultra-focused on ONE ideal client (not "gym owners + realtors + brokers").

Deal room must include:
- Value proposition
- Their problem → why this is the solution
- How you solve it
- Examples/proof of how you solved it
- A **playable demo agent** they can test
- A **chatbot** trained as a "ninja salesperson" (answers + qualifies simultaneously)

---

## 9. The Upsell Ladder (Scale)

```
$47 bootcamp
  → $199–$299 immediate upsell (raise cart value)
    → High-ticket: "build your agent" $5K–$10K–$20K
      → Recurring: maintenance plan $500–$2K/month
        → Mastermind retreats (premium, in-person)
          → Growth Partner: $10K–$20K/month + rev share
```

**Growth partners:** max 10 at a time (fulfillment limit). Become an extension of their team, long-term, revenue-share. Scale through revenue, not more clients.

---

## 10. Sales Calls — when to say yes

Default: handle over email. If they insist on a call, first ask "anything specific you want to talk about?" Most handle over email. If a call is truly needed, it's ≤15 min — pure logistics/trust confirmation, NOT objection handling. They've already self-selected.
