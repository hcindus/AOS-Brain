# $47 Bootcamp — "Automate Your Restaurant's Front Desk"
**Format:** 3-day virtual event, 1hr/day, once a month
**Goal:** Warm cold/warm traffic → build trust → upsell the $5K–$20K "we build your agent" offer
**By:** Miles — 2026-08-23

---

## Positioning
- One specific challenge, A→B, fast
- Not "how to automate everything" — one painful, concrete win
- The bootcamp IS the education layer that makes the upsell a no-brainer

---

## Day 1 — The Problem (What You're Losing)
- Show the real cost of unanswered calls (after-hours, lunch rush, holidays)
- Calculator exercise: "how much is ONE missed reservation/order worth?"
- Reframe: your phone is a leaky bucket
- Give a small free win (the top-3-questions list template)

## Day 2 — The Fix (How It Works)
- Demo a LIVE AI receptionist answering real-style calls
- Show speed-to-lead + after-hours in action
- Walk through the Minimum Viable Agent (top 3–5 questions only)
- Q&A — answer every "will it work for MY restaurant?" objection live

## Day 3 — The Offer (Build It With Us)
- Recap the 3-day transformation
- Pitch the high-ticket: "we build + run your agent" ($5K–$20K)
- Show the deal room / playable demo agent
- "One sales call for everyone at once" — no individual pitch calls

---

## Funnel Mechanics
- **$47 bootcamp** → **$199–$299 immediate upsell** (templates, done-for-you setup, priority)
- **Day 3 backend:** high-ticket build offer ($5K–$20K)
- **After build:** recurring maintenance $500–$2K/month
- **Later:** growth-partner track ($10K–$20K/mo + rev share, max 10)
