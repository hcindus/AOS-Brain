# 7-Email Follow-Up Sequence — PSD AI Voice Agent Offer
**ICP:** Restaurant / bar / retail / beverage operators
**Offer:** "We build + run your AI front-desk (receptionist / after-hours / speed-to-lead)"
**By:** Miles — 2026-08-23

---

## Email 1 — The Download
**Subject:** Here's your [Lead Magnet Name]
- Deliver the resource (link, not PDF — so we can track time-on-page)
- One line: what they'll get, how to use it
- No pitch. Zero selling.

> "Here's the calculator / checklist I promised. It takes ~3 minutes. Pro tip: fill in your call volume from last week first."

---

## Email 2 — Rapport / Track Record
**Subject:** Quick intro + a bit of my story
- Who I am, one-line credibility
- Why I care about THIS problem (their world)
- Subtle: we run what we sell

> "I'm Miles. I've built AI voice agents for restaurants, bars, and retailers — including ones running on our own stack. I got into this because I watched owners lose money to the phone every single day."

---

## Email 3 — Pain-Point Awareness
**Subject:** The after-hours calls you're losing
- Name their #1 challenge (use THEIR language from the OPIN form)
- Cost of inaction, quantified
- No solution yet — just make the pain real

> "Every call that hits voicemail after 9pm is a table or an order walking to the place next door. Most owners don't even know how many they're missing."

---

## Email 4 — Start Converting
**Subject:** The 3 questions an AI receptionist can nail today
- Introduce the Minimum Viable Agent idea (top 3–5 questions)
- Bridge from problem → "here's what's possible"

> "You don't need to automate your whole front desk. Just the top 3 questions you get every day — hours, reservations, takeout. That's 80% of the calls handled instantly."

---

## Email 5 — Convert
**Subject:** Want us to build it for you?
- Present the offer clearly (the high-ticket: "we build your agent")
- Value-based pricing, not feature list
- Clear CTA

> "Here's the offer: we build + configure your AI front-desk, live in 7 days, starting at $8K. You get a demo agent you can call right now."

---

## Email 6 — Testimonial / Proof
**Subject:** How [Client] stopped missing calls
- Real (or early) case study / result
- Third-party validation, not your claims

> "One restaurant we set up went from 40% of after-hours calls going to voicemail to 100% answered. Here's what changed in their first 30 days."

---

## Email 7 — Convert / Last Call
**Subject:** Last call — the door's open
- Scarcity / deadline (real, not fake)
- Remove risk (guarantee / trial)
- Final CTA

> "If this is still on your radar, here's the deal one more time — plus a 30-day money-back guarantee. If not, no hard feelings, I'll leave you with the resource."

---

## Qualification Upgrade: OPIN (replaces BANT)
Ask on the lead-magnet opt-in form:
1. **O**pt-in — email for the magnet
2. **P**ain — "What's your #1 challenge with [calls/front desk] right now?"
3. **I**deal — "What kind of business do you run?" (restaurant / bar / retail / other)
4. **N**ext step — "Want the resource, or want us to build it?"

Their words feed ALL future content + emails ("tool overwhelm", "the tech", "no time").
