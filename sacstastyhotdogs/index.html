Our Story

Good food fast — not fast food.

We opened in 1976 with a simple goal: serve the best hot dogs around. Nearly five decades later, that's still exactly what we do — one fresh, made-with-love hot dog at a time.

Our dogs are a private recipe, prepared just for us by our sausage company and delivered fresh twice a week. Our buns come from an Oakland bakery every single day.

  • 🌭We don't own a freezer — nothing is ever frozen.
  • 🥖Daily-baked buns, delivered fresh each morning.
  • 🌶️Our famous secret-recipe chili, made in-house.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦A family-friendly spot loved by generations.
  • 📚Vintage charm — with kid-friendly books to take home.
