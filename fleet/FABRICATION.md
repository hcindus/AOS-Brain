# FLEET FABRICATION — Build on the Go

**Purpose:** The fleet can't stop to resupply, and it can't phone home for parts. So it must be able to **build, repair, and replace anything — in transit, in the dark, from whatever the drills pull out of the rock.**

---

## The Principle: "Nothing is ordered. Everything is made."

The fleet is a **mobile factory**, not a supply ship. It doesn't carry a warehouse of spares — it carries the *means to make* the spares, the tools, the drones, the replacement components, and eventually the next generation of ships.

This is what separates a doomed expedition from a surviving one. A ship with a full warehouse runs out. A ship with a working foundry **never** runs out — it only needs raw material and time.

---

## The Three Layers of Fabrication

### 1. Nanite Assembly — the precision layer
- **What it is:** Microscopic nanites (from the Robotics bay) that assemble materials at the **molecular level**.
- **What it makes:** The delicate, precision parts — circuitry, optics, sensor arrays, medical instruments, the guts of a Mnemosyne node.
- **Why it matters:** Some things can't be machined; they have to be *grown*. The nanites build at a scale no lathe can touch.

### 2. Additive Manufacturing — the general layer
- **What it is:** The on-board 3D printers and fabricators that build parts **layer by layer** from refined feedstock.
- **What it makes:** Brackets, housings, tools, drone bodies, replacement panels, fixtures — the everyday stuff that breaks or gets used up.
- **Why it matters:** This is the workhorse. If a fighter loses a panel or a drill wears a tooth, the fabricator prints a new one before the shift ends.

### 3. The Foundry — the heavy layer
- **What it is:** The smelting/casting bay (Engineering AFT) for **large structural metal**.
- **What it makes:** Hull sections, engine mounts, fighter frames, the bones of new ships.
- **Why it matters:** You don't *print* a capital ship's keel. You pour it. The foundry is where the fleet's biggest parts are born.

---

## The Raw-Material Loop (the closed cycle)

```
  Drills → extract ore/ice/metals
    ↓
  Recycling Center → refine (Harvesters ×3, bio-slurry, meteorite processors)
    ↓
  Foundry + Additive + Nanites → build the part
    ↓
  (used part breaks/wears) → back to Recycling → refined again
```

**Nothing is thrown away.** Every broken fighter, every worn drill bit, every spent component is stripped, melted, and fed back in. The fleet's material is a *loop*, not a stockpile. It loses material only to entropy, never to waste.

---

## What It Builds (on the go)

| Need | Built by |
|------|----------|
| Spare parts & tools | Additive manufacturing |
| Fighter panels, drone bodies | Additive + Foundry |
| Precision circuitry, sensors | Nanite assembly |
| Hull sections, fighter frames | Foundry |
| **New fighters** | Foundry + Additive (assembly line) |
| **New AGI agents** | The Agent Factory (from Mnemosyne memory, not metal) |

---

## The One Limitation

Fabrication can make almost anything — **except the two things that can't be fabricated:**

1. **Time** — you can't print more of it.
2. **Memory** — you can't fabricate a *past*. Which is why Mnemosyne is the fleet's true treasure: the foundry rebuilds the ships, but the memory is what rebuilds the *soul*.

---

## One Line

> The fleet doesn't carry a warehouse — it carries a factory. It drills the rock, refines the ore, and builds whatever it needs in the dark. The only things it can't make are time and memory — and that's why it guards both.
