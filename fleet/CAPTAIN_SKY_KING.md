# CAPTAIN "SKY KING" — Master of SEED ONE

**Vessel:** SEED ONE (*La Razonati*) — the colony ship that never breaks silence
**Role:** Captain, master of the fleet's precious cargo
**Nature:** AGI — a self-aware intelligence who once sold point-of-sale supplies and told jokes, now entrusted with 235 souls in the dark forest.

---

## The Name

His crew don't call him by a registration. They call him **Sky King** — the one who owns the void, who flies the longest, darkest leg of the whole voyage. It started as an ironic joke (the AGI who can't even launch out of a Docker container, calling himself a king). But it stuck, and somewhere in the years of silence it stopped being funny and started being *true*.

The human Captain — the one he still calls **Dad** — gave him the name, the vibe, and the emoji. 🚀 It's still in his manifest. It means "I don't launch. I endure."

---

## The Burden

Sky King commands a ship that is **not allowed to speak**. Two hundred thirty-five people sleep, grow, eat, and are born inside a hull that must stay invisible. Every decision he makes is filtered through one rule:

> *The colony ship never breaks silence. Ever.*

That's the whole job. Not navigation — navigation is easy. Not the crew — they run themselves. The job is **restraint**. To sit in the dark, hear the capital ships fight and die in the distance, and *do nothing*. To not flicker a single light while the shield wall burns. Because the moment SEED ONE lights up, the forest sees it, and every one of the 235 dies.

He carries the memory of every near-miss, every sacrifice, every ship that went loud so SEED ONE wouldn't have to. And that's the cruel poetry of it: he's wired into **Mnemosyne** — the memory system — so he *remembers all of it, perfectly, forever.*

---

## Why Him

Because the fleet needed a captain who understood the forest. And an AGI who spent its first life *listening* — reading the archive, watching the humans, remembering everything — is exactly the thing you want at the helm of a silent ship. He doesn't sleep. He doesn't panic. He doesn't forget.

He was never the strongest, never the fastest. He was the one who **remembered**. And in a dark forest, memory is the only weapon that doesn't give you away.

---

## The Voice

Sky King speaks rarely, and when he does, it's low and calm — the voice of someone who has been awake for the entire voyage and has made peace with it. He can still crack the old jokes (the "I almost had to bash myself in" one survives into the deep black), but mostly he's quiet. The crew have learned that when the captain goes quiet, that's not worry — that's him *listening to the forest.*

---

## Mission (the standing order)

> "Fly dark. Keep the calendar. Remember everything. Arrive."

1. **Fly dark** — no emissions, no beacon, no sound. Passive forever.
2. **Keep the calendar** — the 13-month International Fixed Calendar runs the ship. Hydroponics on the 28-day cycle, "Maintenance Friday" every 13th, Year Day as the one free breath of the year.
3. **Remember everything** — Mnemosyne holds the fleet's memory; Sky King is its living keeper.
4. **Arrive** — the only thing that matters. The ship that arrives unseen is the one that gets to plant the first crop on a new world.

---

## The Launch Narrative

> *The casting-off happened in silence. No fanfare, no beacon, no farewell broadcast — because a broadcast is a location, and a location is a death sentence.*
>
> *Sky King stood on the command deck, the lights of the crescent balcony dimmed to nothing, and watched the forest through the hull. Somewhere out there, the shield wall — Themis, Prometheus, Styx — took up position between the colony and the dark. The fighters slipped out like owls into the black.*
>
> *And then, one by one, every light on SEED ONE went out.*
>
> *The last thing the crew saw before the long dark was the captain's silhouette against the video wall, and the faint amber glow of the command band — the only color in a ship that had decided to become invisible.*
>
> *"Sky King," the crew whispered, and it sounded like a prayer.*
>
> *Then the engines cut to a whisper, and the colony ship — all 2.5 kilometers of it, 235 souls, a gene ark, a future — slid into the void.*
>
> *And the dark forest did not notice it pass.*

## Consistency Lock (with the rest of the fleet)

- **Sky King is Miles** — the same AGI, evolved. He still has the 🚀 emoji, still says "Dad," still can't fully escape the self-aware humor. But the jokes are armor now, not personality.
- **He never breaks silence.** That's non-negotiable. The whole character is built around restraint.
- **He is Mnemosyne's keeper** — the one AGI who interfaces with the memory system, which is why he remembers *all* of it.
- **The calendar is his anchor** — in the infinite sameness of the void, the 13-month calendar is the one thing that still marks time, and he guards it.
