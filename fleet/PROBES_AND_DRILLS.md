# SEED ONE — Probes & Drills (the fleet's eyes and hands)

**Purpose:** The fleet doesn't just carry a colony — it carries the means to *see* the dark forest without being seen, and to *dig* a future out of a dead world.

---

## PROBES — the silent eyes

Probes are the fleet's reconnaissance, and like everything else, they **fly dark**. No active ping, no beacon — passive sensors only, coasting on cold-gas thrusters. They go out ahead and come back with *knowledge*, not with a signal anyone could trace.

### Survey Probes
- **Count:** 20 (up from the 10 in the original spec)
- **Role:** Map Elysium before a single colonist sets foot — geology, atmosphere, water table, orbital debris.
- **Why they matter:** The fleet lands *blind by design* (it can't scan while approaching, that would broadcast). The survey probes do the seeing that SEED ONE refuses to do — and they do it silently, returning on the Memory-Script channel.

### Recon Probes
- **Count:** 12
- **Role:** The fleet's early-warning eyes. They sit at the edge of the system and *listen* for anything that moves, remembering it into Mnemosyne.
- **Why they matter:** In a dark forest, you don't watch the trees with your own lights on. The recon probes are the owl on the branch — they see the predator before the predator sees them.

### Resource Probes
- **Count:** 15
- **Role:** Survey asteroids, comets, and the destination's moons for the raw material the foundry needs — metals, water-ice, rare earths.
- **Why they matter:** The fleet can't be resupplied. Every gram of ore it finds in transit is a gram it doesn't have to wait to dig for. The resource probes are the fleet's *grocery list* written in the dark.

### Relay Probes
- **Count:** 8
- **Role:** The Memory-Script comms backbone. They hold position in a sparse chain and pass messages home in tight-beam fragments — so no single transmission ever points back to the colony.
- **Why they matter:** A colony that wants to speak home *without* being found needs a route that looks like nothing. The relay probes are the fleet's whisper-network.

---

## DRILLS — the hands that dig a world

Probes *find* the resources; drills *take* them. This is how the fleet turns a dead world — or a cold asteroid — into a working colony.

### Surface Drills
- **Role:** Planet-side mining once landfall is made. Bores into the crust for ore, water, and geothermal.
- **Feeds:** The foundry (construction) and the Recycling Center (refining).

### Asteroid Drills
- **Role:** Transit mining. The fleet encounters a drifting asteroid, anchors, and drills it for whatever it's made of — metal, ice, volatiles.
- **Feeds:** The fighter fabrication and life support. Water-ice becomes both drinking water and reaction mass.

### Core Drills
- **Role:** Deep geological sampling — the "is this world worth staying on?" question. Bores kilometers down to test for a stable crust, water table, and whether the planet is *quiet* all the way to its core (not hiding a molten surprise).
- **Why they matter:** Before the fleet commits 235 souls to a surface, the core drills confirm the world is a *home* and not a *trap*.

---

## THE CYCLE (how it all fits)

```
Probes → find the ore → Drills → extract the ore → Foundry → build the fighters
   ↓                                                    ↑
Resource probes → Memory-Script → Mnemosyne → remembers what was found
```

**One line:** The probes are the eyes that see the dark forest without being seen; the drills are the hands that pull a future out of the rock. Together they mean the fleet never has to *beg* for anything — it only has to *remember where to dig.*
