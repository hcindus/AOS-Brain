Our Story

A Fairfield lunch spot built on one great sandwich.

Joe's Buffet is the kind of place Fairfield locals point you to when you ask "where's the best roast beef in town?" We keep the menu simple and do it right — hand-carved roast beef, fresh rolls, and honest portions.

The star is our Roast Beef Sandwich, piled high on your choice of French roll, sourdough, wheat, sesame, rye, or Dutch crunch. Add a side of au jus to dip, and you've got lunch sorted.

  • 🥩Thinly sliced, hand-carved roast beef — never pre-packed.
  • 🥖Your pick of seven fresh rolls, baked for us daily.
  • 🍖The Joe's Special — roast beef and pastrami, together.
  • 🥗Homestyle sides: vegetable soup, coleslaw & more.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦Family run — a friendly spot loved by generations.
