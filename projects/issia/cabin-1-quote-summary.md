## Cross-Check Findings

### ✅ Clean
- All extended-cost arithmetic reconciles exactly to the stated total ($21,854.21).
- The consolidated "Item List" at the bottom matches the sum of section quantities (track 442.91 LF, studs 166.09 EA, drywall 55.36 sheets, insulation 2,214.54 SF) — no drift.

### ⚠️ Findings (3)

1. **Aggregate Base — duplicate/conflicting entry.**
   Two lines: `Aggregate Base @ $0/Ton × 11.16 Ton = $0` AND `Aggregate Base @ $55/Cu Yd × 15 CY = $825` (flagged "???"). These appear to be the same material priced in two different units (Ton vs Cubic Yards) — one unpriced, one priced. **Recommendation:** reconcile to a single unit (likely CY for a slab base) and confirm quantity; the $825 may be either correct or a double-count.

2. **Labor is entirely unpriced.**
   "Concrete Flatwork Labor" (843.68 SF), "Drywall Finish @ Walls — Level 4" (1,326.87 + 1,372.70 SF) all carry **$0 unit cost**. The $21,854 total is **materials-only**. Concrete flatwork + Level-4 drywall finishing across ~2,700 SF is typically several thousand dollars. **Real all-in cost is meaningfully above $21,854.**

3. **Stated project range is already exceeded.**
   The sheet's own note reads "Project cost: $9,753 – $20,392," but materials alone total $21,854 — ~7% over the top of that range *before* labor and the aggregate-base reconciliation.
