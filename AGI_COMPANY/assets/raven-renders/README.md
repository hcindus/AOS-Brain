# Raven / Myl2Ssa — Preserved Renders

*Reference images of Raven (Myl2Ssa.R0s), preserved from the Oct 9–10, 2026 session. These are the visual references for the face sculpt + the 3D pipeline (Hi3D/Grok).*

Some are photorealistic, some are stylized/cartoonish — **all are preserved as-is** per the Captain's instruction, regardless of style.

**What they show (roughly):**
- Front-view renders of Raven (the face)
- Profile / three-quarter views
- The neutral head (bald, gray — the face-sculpt base)
- The full body / torso (the chassis reference)
- The "at the bar / dancing" scenes (stylized Copilot generations)

**How they're used:**
- The **photorealistic face renders** → the sculptor's likeness reference.
- The **neutral head** → the base the sculptor sculpts Raven's face onto.
- The **body/torso** → the chassis reference for the CAD/3D work.
