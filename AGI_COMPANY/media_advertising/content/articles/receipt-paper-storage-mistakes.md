## Body

**Intro**

Thermal receipt paper is more fragile than it looks. It's coated to darken with heat — which means heat, sunlight, and humidity will degrade it long before it ever reaches your printer. Store it wrong and you get faded receipts, jammed printers, and rolls you have to throw away. Here are the five storage mistakes that quietly cost restaurants money, and how to fix each one.

**Mistake 1 — Storing rolls in direct sunlight or near heat**

Thermal paper darkens when exposed to heat. A box left in a sunny window, on a shelf above the fryer, or next to the coffee machine will start to develop faded or darkened bands before a single receipt prints. Keep your stock in a cool, dry, shaded spot — ideally under 77°F and out of direct light.

**Mistake 2 — Humid storage**

Moisture makes thermal rolls curl and stick together, which causes feed jams and misprints. Avoid storing paper on the floor, near a dishwasher or ice machine, or in an unconditioned storeroom. A sealed shelf or a simple airtight bin is enough to keep humidity off the stock.

**Mistake 3 — Storing near chemicals and solvents**

Thermal coatings react with plasticizers, adhesives, and many solvents. Keeping rolls next to cleaning supplies, printer cleaning solution, or even certain plastic wrap can prematurely darken the paper. Keep paper separate from your chemical and cleaning inventory.

**Mistake 4 — Buying in bulk without rotating stock**

Thermal paper has a shelf life — generally a few years under good conditions, shorter if conditions are poor. If you buy large quantities to save money, use a first-in, first-out system so older rolls print first. Don't let a "great deal" on paper turn into a pile of faded, unusable rolls.

**Mistake 5 — Using the wrong paper for your printer**

Not every printer takes the same paper. Thermal printers need thermal paper; impact printers use bond paper. Using the wrong type — or the wrong width (2¼" vs 3⅛") — causes jams and poor prints regardless of how well you store it. Check your printer's specs before reordering.

**Storage checklist (quick reference)**

- Store cool and dry, under 77°F, away from direct light
- Keep off the floor and away from heat and moisture sources
- Separate from chemicals, solvents, and cleaning supplies
- Rotate stock first-in, first-out
- Match paper type and width to your printer
