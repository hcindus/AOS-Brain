# PSD Agent Catalog — AI Agents for Local Businesses

**Purpose:** A sellable catalog of AI agents that local businesses (restaurants, retail, salons, service shops) can deploy to handle their front-office and follow-up work.

**Tone:** Each entry answers: *what it does, what the owner gets, and why they'd pay for it.*

---

## Secretarial Pool (front-office / reception)

| Agent | What it does | The owner gets |
|-------|-------------|----------------|
| **Greeter** | Welcomes customers, answers "are you open?", basic FAQs, first-touch engagement | A front door that never sleeps, answers instantly |
| **Closeter** | Follows up warm leads, handles objections, gets the "yes" | More booked jobs / closed sales |
| **Concierge** | Answers questions, makes recommendations, schedules | A 24/7 helper that knows the menu/services |
| **Clerk** | Takes messages, logs requests, files records, data entry | No lost messages, clean records |
| **Assistant** | General admin — reminders, follow-ups, light scheduling | The "remembering" off your plate |
| **Executive Assistant** | Deep scheduling, ops coordination, reporting (Jordan-class) | A right-hand that runs the back office |

---

## Customer-Facing Agents (reputation + responsiveness)

| Agent | What it does | The owner gets |
|-------|-------------|----------------|
| **Review Agent** | Monitors + responds to Google/Yelp reviews, politely and on-brand | Better reputation, more trust, higher search rank |
| **Missed-Call Responder** | Auto-replies to missed calls via text/voicemail so no lead is lost | Every missed call becomes a follow-up |
| **Telegram Ping Agent** | Pings the owner instantly on new leads/orders/events | Real-time awareness, zero lag |
