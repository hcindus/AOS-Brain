# SI, Not AI

**The one-liner:**

---

**The moment.**

In November 2025, Yann LeCun — one of the three "godfathers" of deep learning, and Meta's chief AI scientist for 12 years — walked out and raised **$1.03 billion** to build "world models over language models." His company, AMI Labs, has **no product and no revenue**; the $3.5B valuation is pure conviction. Jeff Bezos, Eric Schmidt, Mark Cuban, Nvidia, Samsung, and Toyota all wrote checks.

He is not alone. Ilia Sutskever (OpenAI co-founder) and Fei-Fei Li (World Labs) each made the same bet separately — a billion dollars each. And Sam Altman, the man who built the LLM gold rush, just walked back publicly: **"we need a whole new architecture."**

The consensus is shifting, quietly but unmistakably. In 2022, **1 in 10** AI papers studied LLM limitations. By 2025, it was **1 in 3**.

---

**The argument (LeCun's, in one breath).**

LLMs predict the next word unbelievably well — but they live entirely in *discrete text world.* They have no model of the physical world inside them. Moravec's paradox: the stuff easy for humans (catching a ball, walking stairs, not spilling coffee) is impossibly hard for machines, and vice versa. You can't scale your way to common sense.

Real intelligence needs a **world model** — grounded in perception, physics, and embodiment, learned the way a baby learns gravity: *by dropping things, not by reading about dropping things.*

- **The AOS brain is a body, not a text predictor.** Heart, stomach, lungs, liver, kidneys, thyroid — ternary organs with continuity, persistence, and a live signal/noise pipeline. That is embodiment. That is a world model.

- **The Dark Factory builds agents that *do*.** Validate → allocate → build → verify → blind hold-out → deploy. Agents that act in the world, not agents that generate plausible-sounding text.
