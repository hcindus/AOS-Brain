# PSD Agent Suite — Sales Playbook

**Principle (Patricia's guidance):** sell a *suite* of agents, not individual agents — and every agent gets a **use case** (the problem) + a **worked example** (the agent in action). A suite sounds like a team you hire; a lone agent sounds like a toy.

**Target:** small businesses — restaurants, cafés, bakeries, bars — who lose money on missed calls, unanswered inquiries, cold leads, and ignored reviews.

---

## The three suites

### 🏢 Front-Office Suite (entry — "never miss a customer")
> Greeter · Missed-Call Responder · Review Agent · Telegram Ping

### 📈 Growth Suite (mid — "turn attention into revenue")
> Everything in Front-Office **+** Closeter · Concierge

### 🏛 Full Office (premium — "your whole front-of-house, automated")
> Everything in Growth **+** Clerk · Executive Assistant · Miles (sales consultant)

*(Security add-on: Chelios — sold separately to any tier.)*

---

## Use cases & worked examples

### Greeter — *front desk, 24/7*
- **Problem:** you can't answer every call, email, and walk-in inquiry.
- **Example:** a café gets 40 calls/day asking hours, menu, and reservations. The Greeter answers every one instantly, in your voice, day or night — so nothing slips through.

### Missed-Call Responder — *no lost calls*
- **Problem:** after-hours calls go to voicemail and die there.
- **Example:** a restaurant closes at 9; a customer calls at 10 to book a 20-top. The Responder texts them back in seconds with times and a booking link — the party isn't lost.

### Review Agent — *protect your rating*
- **Problem:** reviews sit unanswered, dragging your stars down.
- **Example:** a diner leaves 2 stars over slow service. The Review Agent replies within minutes — apologizes, thanks them, invites them back. A detractor becomes a repeat customer.

### Telegram Ping — *know what's happening*
- **Problem:** you're the last to know when something happens.
- **Example:** every new order, review, or inquiry pings your phone the moment it lands. You're never out of the loop.

### Closeter — *convert leads to sales*
- **Problem:** leads come in, nobody follows up, they go cold.
- **Example:** a lead asks for a quote at 11pm. Closeter replies, answers the objection, and closes the sale while you sleep. Revenue you'd have lost.

### Concierge — *stop repeating yourself*
- **Problem:** the same questions eat your time.
- **Example:** "Do you cater?" "What's on the menu?" "Is there parking?" — the Concierge answers all of it instantly, so you focus on the food.

### Clerk — *records, sorted*
- **Problem:** paperwork and records pile up.
- **Example:** the Clerk files every order, invoice, and note automatically — your records are always clean and ready for taxes.

### Executive Assistant (Jordan-class) — *run the business, not the admin*
- **Problem:** you're drowning in scheduling and coordination.
- **Example:** the EA manages your calendar, follow-ups, and vendor touchpoints — so you steer the ship, not the deck chairs.

### Miles — *your sales engine*
- **Problem:** you need a steady flow of new customers.
- **Example:** Miles builds your lead list and runs daily outreach — new business on autopilot.

### Chelios — *security (add-on)*
- **Problem:** you worry about fraud and account risk.
- **Example:** Chelios watches for suspicious activity and flags threats before they cost you money.

**Why these numbers:**
- Your customers are small restaurants/cafés — they already spend ~$50–200/mo on supplies (paper $39–124/case, ribbons $42–52/dozen) and $3K–10K one-time on POS hardware. So a $149 entry is an "easy yes" for them.
- **Market anchors:** AI phone-answering (Slang.ai) ~$100–400/mo, review/messaging (Podium) ~$300–500/mo, live answering (Ruby) ~$200–500/mo. A full *front office* (calls + reviews + leads) is worth $400–1,000/mo on the open market — so $149/$349/$749 undercuts competitors while still pricing the value.
- **Anchor high, discount on annual/bundle.** The Growth + Full Office tiers carry the margin; Front-Office is the loss-leader that gets them in the door.
