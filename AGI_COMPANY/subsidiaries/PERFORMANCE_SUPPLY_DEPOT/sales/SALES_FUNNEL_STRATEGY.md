## What is a Sales Funnel?

A sales funnel is the step-by-step process that guides potential customers from first awareness to final purchase. Think of it like a real funnel: wide at the top (many prospects), narrowing as people drop off at each stage, with buyers emerging at the bottom.

### The 4 Stages of Our Funnel:

| Stage | Name | Goal | Content Type |
|-------|------|------|--------------|
| **Top** | Awareness | Get noticed | Educational content, social media |
| **Middle** | Interest | Build trust | Detailed info, comparisons, case studies |
| **Bottom** | Decision | Drive action | Offers, demos, testimonials |
| **Base** | Retention | Keep them | Service, upsells, referrals |
