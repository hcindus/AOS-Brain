## 1. FINCEN MSB REGISTRATION REQUIREMENTS

### Overview
Under 31 CFR § 1022.380, any business operating as a Money Services Business must register with the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN), a bureau of the U.S. Department of Treasury.

### Registration Requirements
- **Who Must Register:** Any person engaging in money transmission, check cashing, or currency dealing/exchange
- **Exemption Threshold:** Operating as an agent of another MSB does not require separate registration
- **Foreign-Located Businesses:** Must designate a U.S. agent for service of legal process

### Registration Process
| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Form** | FinCEN Form 107 (MSB Registration) |
| **Filing Method** | Online via BSA E-Filing System |
| **Initial Deadline** | Within 180 days of business establishment |
| **Renewal Period** | Every 2 years (biennial renewal) |
| **Renewal Deadline** | By December 31 of renewal year |

### Required Information
1. Legal name, trade names, and DBA names
2. Employer Identification Number (EIN)
3. Business address and mailing address
4. Contact information
5. Type of MSB activities (money transmission, currency dealing, etc.)
6. Bank account information
7. List of agents (if applicable)
8. Number of branches/locations

### Penalties for Non-Compliance
- Civil penalty: **$5,000 per violation** (each day = separate violation)
- Criminal penalties under 18 U.S.C. § 1960
- Potential for business closure

### Cost Estimate
| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Registration Fee | **FREE** |
| Legal/Compliance Assistance | $5,000 - $15,000 |

## 2. ANTI-MONEY LAUNDERING (AML) PROGRAM REQUIREMENTS

### 31 CFR § 1022.210 Requirements
Every MSB must develop, implement, and maintain an effective written AML program:

### Program Components
1. **Policies, Procedures & Internal Controls**
   - Customer identification/verification (CIP)
   - Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) filing procedures
   - Currency Transaction Report (CTR) procedures for transactions >$10,000
   - Record retention (5-year requirement)

2. **Compliance Officer Designation**
   - Must designate a person responsible for day-to-day compliance
   - Cannot be the same person conducting independent reviews

3. **Training Program**
   - Regular training on AML/BSA requirements
   - Detection of suspicious transactions

4. **Independent Review**
   - Annual independent testing of AML program
   - May be conducted by internal personnel (not compliance officer)

### Reporting Requirements
| Report Type | Threshold | Filing Deadline |
|-------------|-----------|-----------------|
| **SAR** | $2,000+ suspicious activity | Within 30 days |
| **CTR** | $10,000+ cash transaction | Within 15 days |
| **FBAR** | $10,000+ aggregate foreign accounts | April 15 annually |

### Cost Estimate
| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| AML Program Development | $10,000 - $25,000 |
| Annual Independent Review | $5,000 - $15,000 |
| Compliance Software | $15,000 - $50,000/year |
| Compliance Officer Salary | $75,000 - $150,000/year |

## 4. NEW YORK BITLICENSE REQUIREMENTS

### Overview
The BitLicense is a special business license required for virtual currency activities in New York State, issued by the New York Department of Financial Services (DFS).

### 23 NYCRR Part 200 Requirements

### Who Needs a BitLicense
- Receiving virtual currency for transmission
- Maintaining custody of virtual currency on behalf of others
- Buying/selling virtual currency as a customer business
- Issuing virtual currency
- Performing exchange services

### Application Requirements
1. **Application Form** - Detailed application to DFS
2. **Background Checks** - Fingerprints and background for all principals
3. **Financial Statements** - Audited financials demonstrating sufficient net worth
4. **Business Plan** - Detailed operations, compliance, and risk management plan
5. **Compliance Policies** - Comprehensive BSA/AML, KYC, and cybersecurity policies
6. **Capital Requirements** - Varies based on business model; typically $500,000+
7. **Surety Bond** - Minimum $500,000, may be higher based on volume
8. **Cybersecurity Program** - Detailed security framework
9. **Consumer Protection** - Policies for handling consumer complaints

### Timeline
- **Typical Processing Time:** 12-24 months
- **Fastest Cases:** 6-12 months (rare)

### Fees

## 5. SECURITIES LAW CONSIDERATIONS (HOWEY TEST)

### Overview
Cryptocurrencies may be classified as securities under federal law, requiring SEC registration or exemption.

### The Howey Test (SEC v. W.J. Howey Co., 1946)
An instrument is a security if it involves:
1. **Investment of Money**
2. **Common Enterprise**
3. **Expectation of Profits**
4. **Derived from Efforts of Others**

### Application to DUSTY
| Factor | Risk Assessment |
|--------|-----------------|
| Investment of Money | HIGH - Customers deposit funds |
| Common Enterprise | MEDIUM - Pooling of assets |
| Expectation of Profits | LOW-MEDIUM - Payment processing vs. investment |
| Efforts of Others | LOW - Users control their funds |

### Securities Law Compliance Options

#### Option A: Avoid Security Classification
- Structure as pure payment processing (custody only)
- No investment features or yield products
- No commingling of customer funds with proprietary funds
- Clear terms of service defining custodial relationship

#### Option B: Registration (if deemed security)
- SEC Registration as Money Transmitter (not typical)
- State Blue Sky registrations
- **Estimated Cost:** $250,000 - $1,000,000+

### Recommended Approach
Structure DUSTY as a **custodial payment processor** rather than an investment vehicle:
- Clear segregation of customer funds
- No interest-bearing accounts
- No investment advice or recommendations
- Regular compliance reviews

## 6. BANKING PARTNERSHIPS

### Overview
Cryptocurrency businesses face significant challenges obtaining traditional banking relationships due to "de-risking" concerns.

### Types of Banking Relationships Needed

#### 1. Operating Account
- Standard business checking account
- Payroll and operational expenses
- **Challenge:** Many banks refuse crypto businesses

#### 2. Custody/Segregated Accounts
- Holding customer fiat funds
- Must be separate from operating funds
- **Requirement:** Many states require this for MTL

#### 3. Processing Account
- ACH/wire processing capabilities
- Merchant services for card payments
- **Challenge:** High-risk merchant category
