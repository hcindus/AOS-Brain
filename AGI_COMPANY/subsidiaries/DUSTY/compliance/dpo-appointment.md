## 3. DPO RESPONSIBILITIES

### 3.1 Core Duties

1. **Privacy Policy Oversight**
   - Maintain and update Privacy Policy
   - Ensure compliance with GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and other regulations
   - Respond to data subject requests (access, deletion, portability)

2. **Compliance Monitoring**
   - Monitor data processing activities
   - Conduct regular privacy impact assessments (PIAs)
   - Track regulatory changes and update policies

3. **Data Breach Response**
   - Lead breach investigation and response
   - Notify supervisory authorities within 72 hours (GDPR)
   - Notify affected individuals when required

4. **Staff Training**
   - Develop and deliver privacy training
   - Maintain training records
   - Conduct annual privacy awareness programs

5. **Vendor Management**
   - Review and approve Data Processing Agreements (DPAs)
   - Audit third-party data processors
   - Ensure vendor compliance

### 3.2 CCPA/CPRA Specific Duties

- Respond to consumer rights requests within 45 days
- Maintain "Do Not Sell" mechanism
- Update privacy notices as required
- Coordinate with legal on regulatory inquiries

### 3.3 GDPR Specific Duties

- Maintain Records of Processing Activities (ROPA)
- Cooperate with supervisory authorities
- Advise organization on Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs)
- Serve as point of contact for data subjects and regulators
