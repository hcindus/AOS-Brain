## 6. Risk Management Requirements

To obtain coverage and minimize premiums:

### Security Measures

- [ ] Multi-signature wallets (2-of-3 minimum)
- [ ] Hardware security modules (HSMs)
- [ ] Regular penetration testing
- [ ] Incident response plan
- [ ] Employee background checks

### Documentation

- [ ] Security policies
- [ ] Compliance procedures
- [ ] Disaster recovery plan
- [ ] Vendor due diligence

## 7. Claims Procedures

### Immediate Steps

1. **Secure systems** - Stop ongoing loss
2. **Document incident** - Preserve evidence
3. **Notify carrier** - Within 24 hours
4. **Cooperate** - Provide requested information
5. **Mitigate** - Minimize further damage
