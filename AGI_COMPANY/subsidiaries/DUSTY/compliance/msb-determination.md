# MSB Status Determination

**Company:** Performance Supply Depot LLC d/b/a Dusty  
**Date:** July 3, 2026  
**Document:** Formal Determination

---

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**DETERMINATION:** Dusty operates as a **Money Services Business (MSB)** under FinCEN regulations.

**Rationale:** Dusty provides cryptocurrency payment processing services that involve:
- Transmission of virtual currency (crypto payments)
- Acceptance and transmission of funds (payment processing)
- Activities exceeding $1,000 per day per person (threshold exceeded)

**Status:** Custodial (holds crypto temporarily during transactions)

---

## 2. REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

### 2.1 FinCEN Guidance

**31 CFR § 1010.100(ff)(8) - Money Services Business Definition:**

An MSB includes any person doing business, whether or not on a regular basis or as an organized business concern, in whole or in part, as:

- A money transmitter
- An issuer of money orders or traveler's checks
- A seller or redeemer of money orders or traveler's checks
- A check casher
- A provider of prepaid access

### 2.2 Virtual Currency Specific

**FinCEN Guidance FIN-2013-G001:**

> "An administrator or exchanger that (1) accepts and transmits a convertible virtual currency or (2) buys or sells convertible virtual currency for any reason is a money transmitter under FinCEN's regulations, unless a limitation to or exemption from the definition applies."

**FIN-2019-G001:** Confirms custodial wallets are money transmission.

---

## 3. DUSTY ACTIVITIES ANALYSIS

### 3.1 Services Provided

| Service | Description | MSB Activity |
|---------|-------------|--------------|
| Crypto Payment Processing | Accept crypto from payers, transmit to recipients | ✅ Money Transmission |
| Payment Facilitation | Process payments between parties | ✅ Money Transmission |
| Wallet Services | Temporary custody during transactions | ✅ Money Transmission |
| Compliance Monitoring | AML/KYC screening | ❌ Ancillary (if separate) |

### 3.2 Custodial vs Non-Custodial

**Determination: CUSTODIAL**

| Factor | Custodial | Non-Custodial |
|--------|-----------|---------------|
| Dusty holds private keys | ✅ YES | NO |
| User controls funds | NO | ✅ YES |
| Dusty can move funds unilaterally | ✅ YES | NO |
| Transaction execution | Dusty executes | User executes |

**Evidence:**
- Dusty operates exchange accounts with API keys
- Funds flow through Dusty-controlled wallets
- Dusty executes transactions on behalf of users
- Users do not maintain direct control of private keys

### 3.3 Threshold Analysis

**31 CFR § 1010.100(ff)(8)(iii) - Money Transmitter Threshold:**

> Transmission of funds exceeding $1,000 per day per person

**Dusty Analysis:**
- Average transaction: $5,000-$50,000
- Daily volume: Exceeds threshold
- Per-person analysis: Individual transactions exceed $1,000

**Conclusion:** Threshold EXCEEDED

---

## 4. EXEMPTIONS ANALYZED

### 4.1 Payment Processor Exemption

**31 CFR § 1010.100(ff)(8)(iii)(B):**

A person is not a money transmitter if:
- Accepts and transmits funds only integral to sale of goods/services
- Provides goods/services in exchange for funds

**Dusty Assessment:**
- ❌ Does NOT qualify - Dusty does not sell goods/services
- ❌ Provides payment processing as standalone service

### 4.2 Prepaid Access Exemption

**31 CFR § 1010.100(ff)(4):**

Does not apply - Dusty does not issue prepaid access products.

### 4.3 Registered MSB Agent

**31 CFR § 1010.100(ff)(8)(iii)(C):**

Dusty is NOT an agent of another MSB - acts as principal.

---

## 5. CONCLUSION

### 5.1 Determination

**Dusty IS a Money Services Business (MSB)**

Specifically:
- **Money Transmitter** under 31 CFR § 1010.100(ff)(8)(iii)
- **Custodial** virtual currency wallet provider
- **Subject to** BSA/AML compliance requirements
