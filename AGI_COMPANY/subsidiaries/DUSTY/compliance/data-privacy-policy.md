## 3. Legal Basis for Processing

### 3.1 CCPA (California)

We collect personal information for:
- Providing services (contractual necessity)
- Legal compliance (tax, AML, securities laws)
- Legitimate business interests (security, fraud prevention)

**Your CCPA Rights:**
- Know what personal information we collect
- Know how we use and share it
- Request deletion (subject to legal holds)
- Opt-out of sale (we do not sell data)
- Non-discrimination for exercising rights

### 3.2 GDPR (EU/UK)

For users in the EU/UK, we process data based on:
- Contract performance
- Legal obligations
- Legitimate interests
- Consent (where required)

**Your GDPR Rights:**
- Access your data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Restrict processing
- Data portability
- Object to processing
- Withdraw consent
