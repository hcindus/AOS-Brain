## Tier 1: Direct Competitors

### BuiltWith
- **Revenue:** $14M/year (estimated)
- **Employees:** 4
- **Model:** SaaS subscription + API
- **Data:** Technology detection (web frameworks, analytics, hosting)
- **Positioning:** "Find out what websites are built with"
- **Pricing:** $295/mo (Basic) → $995/mo (Enterprise)
- **Strengths:** Massive database, accurate detection, API-first
- **Weaknesses:** Web-only, no physical POS detection, no contact enrichment
- **Our Angle:** Physical POS + local businesses + contact data

### ZoomInfo
- **Revenue:** $3.2B/year
- **Employees:** 3,000+
- **Model:** Enterprise sales intelligence
- **Data:** B2B contacts, org charts, intent signals
- **Positioning:** "The go-to-market platform"
- **Pricing:** $10,000+/year (enterprise only)
- **Strengths:** Massive contact database, integrations, enterprise focus
- **Weaknesses:** Expensive, SMBs excluded, not POS-specific
- **Our Angle:** SMB-friendly pricing, POS-specific intelligence, California focus

### SEMrush / Ahrefs
- **Revenue:** ~$400M/year combined
- **Model:** SEO/marketing intelligence
- **Data:** Keywords, backlinks, competitor analysis
- **Positioning:** "Grow your online visibility"
- **Pricing:** $119/mo → $449/mo
- **Strengths:** SEO data quality, content marketing tools
- **Weaknesses:** Web-only, no physical business data, not sales-focused
- **Our Angle:** Offline business intelligence + sales outreach data

## Market Gaps We're Filling

### 1. **Physical POS Detection**
No competitor detects in-store equipment (Square terminal, Toast handheld, Clover station).
- Method: AI analysis of Google Business photos, review text mining

### 2. **SMB-Friendly Pricing**
ZoomInfo starts at $10K/year. BuiltWith at $295/mo.
- Our entry: $97/mo for county-level access
- Target: Individual sales reps, small service companies

### 3. **Replacement Timing Intelligence**
AI predicts when businesses are likely upgrading equipment based on:
- Equipment age estimation from photo analysis
- Transaction volume trends (public health inspections)
- License renewal dates

### 4. **California Depth**
Start with California mastery vs. national thin coverage:
- 58 counties
- 75,000+ licensed establishments
- Weekly refresh
