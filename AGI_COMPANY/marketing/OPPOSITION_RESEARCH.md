# Opposition Research: Customer Pain Points
## Performance Supply Depot Marketing Intelligence

**Research Date:** 2026-04-28  
**Strategy:** Highlight pain, never ascribe blame  
**Goal:** Position PSD as the solution to industry-wide friction

---

## The Philosophy

> *"The customer doesn't care who's causing the problem. They care that the problem exists, and that you can solve it."*

We never name competitors in our marketing. We identify universal pain points that customers experience, then show how Performance Supply Depot eliminates them.

---

## Research Methodology

1. **Review mining** - Analyze 500+ customer reviews across POS supply vendors
2. **Social listening** - Monitor Reddit r/restaurantowners, r/smallbusiness
3. **Direct interviews** - 12 Vegas restaurant managers (pain discovery)
4. **Support ticket analysis** - Common complaints from industry forums

---

## Universal Pain Points (The Real Opposition)

### Pain Point #1: The Inventory Panic

**The Experience:**
> "It's 10pm on a Friday. We're slammed. The printer runs out of paper. The backup roll I thought was there... isn't. Now we're handwriting receipts and the line is out the door."

**The Friction:**
- Emergency supply runs during peak hours
- "Just-in-time" delivery that arrives just-too-late
- No visibility into stock levels
- Staff confusion about where supplies are stored

**The PSD Solution:**
- Same-day delivery in Vegas (we're local)
- Auto-reorder reminders based on your usage
- Stock verification on every delivery
- Clear labeling system for storage

**Marketing Message:**
> "Your printer never gives warning at 2pm on Tuesday. It waits until Friday at 8pm when you're three deep. We get it. That's why we're here until 10pm with same-day delivery."

---

### Pain Point #2: The Mystery Product Problem

**The Experience:**
> "I ordered thermal paper. What showed up doesn't fit my printer. Now I'm on hold with support, and they want me to read a SKU number that's printed in 2pt font on the bottom of the box."

**The Friction:**
- Generic "universal" products that fit nothing universally
- SKU confusion between models
- No one to ask before ordering
- Return process takes 2+ weeks

**The PSD Solution:**
- "Show us your printer" - send a photo, we identify the right supplies
- Guaranteed fit or free replacement
- Local support answers the phone
- 30-day hassle-free returns

**Marketing Message:**
> "Thermal paper comes in 47 sizes. Your printer takes one of them. We'll help you find that one. (And if we get it wrong, we'll make it right before your next shift.)"

---

### Pain Point #3: The Ghost Invoice

**The Experience:**
> "I'm doing books on Sunday. There's a $400 charge I don't recognize. No invoice email. No phone call. Just... money gone. Now I spend my Sunday chasing ghosts."

**The Friction:**
- Surprise charges appearing on statements
- Difficulty reaching billing support
- Auto-renewal traps
- Hidden "processing fees"

**The PSD Solution:**
- Clear pricing upfront (what you see is what you pay)
- Text/email confirmations on every order
- No auto-billing without explicit opt-in
- Local account manager you can call

**Marketing Message:**
> "Your Sunday is for family, not forensic accounting. Every charge from us comes with a text confirmation and a receipt you can actually find."

---

### Pain Point #4: The Shipping Calculus

**The Experience:**
> "The paper is $89. Shipping is $47. But if I order $150, shipping is free. So now I'm doing math at 11pm trying to figure out what else I need to hit the threshold. I'm a restaurant manager, not a cart optimizer."

**The Friction:**
- Free shipping thresholds that require overbuying
- Shipping costs that double the order total
- Slow shipping on essentials (3-5 days for receipt paper?)
- Expedited shipping that costs more than the product

**The PSD Solution:**
- Flat-rate delivery in Vegas ($9.99, any size order)
- Same-day available (not expedited - just... our normal)
- No minimum order games
- You order what you need, when you need it

**Marketing Message:**
> "You shouldn't need a calculator to order receipt paper. $9.99 flat delivery anywhere in Vegas. Same day if you order by 3pm. That's it."

---

### Pain Point #5: The Health Question Nobody Asked

**The Experience:**
> "My server just got diagnosed with something. Doctor asked if she handles receipts. I said yes, thousands per week. He mentioned BPA. I'd never even thought about it."

**The Friction:**
- BPA/BPS in standard thermal paper
- Staff handling 200+ receipts daily
- No information about what's in the supplies
- Health-conscious businesses feeling stuck

**The PSD Solution:**
- Full phenol-free (not just BPA-free) options
- Staff safety as a selling point
- California Prop 65 compliant
- Same price as standard paper

**Marketing Message:**
> "Your staff handles 200 receipts a day. They deserve to know what's in them. Our phenol-free paper is the same price, same reliability, minus the chemicals."

---

### Pain Point #6: The Support Maze

**The Experience:**
> "I have a question about my printer. I call support. They ask for my account number. I give it. They transfer me. New person asks for account number. Then they tell me I need technical support and transfer me again. Third person asks for account number. I hang up."

**The Friction:**
- Phone trees that go 7 levels deep
- Outsourced support reading scripts
- Getting bounced between departments
- No one who knows your business

**The PSD Solution:**
- One phone number, human answers
- Local team knows Vegas restaurant scene
- Technical support included (we know POS systems)
- Account manager remembers your name

**Marketing Message:**
> "When you call us, you get Miles. Or someone who knows your setup. No phone trees. No scripts. No transfers. Just answers."

## Competitor Analysis (Internal Use Only)

### WebstaurantStore
- **Strength:** Huge selection, competitive pricing
- **Friction:** Shipping costs, generic support, 3-5 day delivery
- **Their customers say:** "Great prices if you plan a week ahead"

### Amazon
- **Strength:** Fast Prime delivery, easy ordering
- **Friction:** Quality inconsistency, counterfeit products, no technical support
- **Their customers say:** "Hit or miss on whether it's the right paper"

### Uline
- **Strength:** Business-focused, fast shipping
- **Friction:** High minimums, impersonal, limited POS selection
- **Their customers say:** "Good for boxes, not for printers"

### Terminal Depot / POSPaper
- **Strength:** POS specialization, decent pricing
- **Friction:** Slow shipping, generic support, no local presence
- **Their customers say:** "Know their stuff, but I'm in NYC and they're in Ohio"

## Tactical Recommendations

### Immediate Actions

1. **Create "Pain Point" Landing Pages**
   - `/last-minute-delivery` - Address the Friday night panic
   - `/will-it-fit` - Address the mystery product problem
   - `/no-surprises` - Address ghost invoice fear

2. **Email Campaigns by Pain**
   - Subject: "That 10pm feeling..." (Inventory panic)
   - Subject: "Your printer + our paper = guaranteed fit" (Mystery product)
   - Subject: "Sunday is for family, not forensic accounting" (Ghost invoice)

3. **Social Content**
   - "POV: It's Friday at 8pm and you're out of paper" (relatable pain)
   - "When you order thermal paper but get a geometry lesson" (SKU confusion)
   - "Receipts your staff actually wants to touch" (health angle)

### Long-term Strategy

1. **"Pain Discovery" Sales Calls**
   - Don't sell. Ask: "What's your biggest supply headache?"
   - Document the answers
   - Create content around each pain

2. **Case Studies Without Names**
   - "Local pizza shop was doing emergency supply runs 3x/week"
   - "Diner saved $200/month just by fixing the 'wrong paper' problem"
   - "Server health concerns led them to phenol-free"

3. **Competitor Mention Policy**
   - Never name them in public
   - Never disparage them
   - Focus on the customer's pain
   - Let the customer connect the dots
