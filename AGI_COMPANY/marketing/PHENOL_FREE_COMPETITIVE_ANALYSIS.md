## Market Overview: Phenol-Free Thermal Paper

### What is Phenol-Free Thermal Paper?
- **Also called:** BPA-free, phenol-free, health-conscious thermal paper
- **Why it matters:** Traditional thermal paper contains BPA (Bisphenol A), a chemical linked to health concerns
- **Regulations:** 
  - EU banned BPA in thermal paper (2016)
  - California Prop 65 requires warnings on BPA-containing products
  - Growing consumer demand for safer alternatives
- **Premium Product:** Typically 20-40% more expensive than standard thermal paper

### Key Differentiators
- **BPA-Free:** No Bisphenol A
- **BPS-Free:** Some "BPA-free" papers use BPS (Bisphenol S), also controversial
- **Phenol-Free:** Free of all phenol compounds (BPA, BPS, BPF, etc.)
- **Health-conscious:** Safer for staff handling receipts daily
