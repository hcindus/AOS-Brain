## Notes (what the AI gets wrong — watch for these)

1. **"Cybernetic suit" ≠ exposed body.** AI smooths "human face + mechanical body" into "person wearing armor." If the render reads as a *costume*, add:
   > *"She is not wearing armor or a suit — her body IS the machine. No clothing, no costume, no plating. The mechanical skeleton is her actual anatomy."*
2. **"Fleshy chest" trips content filters** (flagged as NSFW). We keep the language for our own use; if a tool blocks it, that's the filter, not the design.
3. **Setting drift** — it likes to add a "futuristic lab"; we want "dark studio background."

## Variants
- **Back view** → replace "Front view (straight-on)" with "Back view" (shows the spine + tendons).
- **Male** (if ever) → swap hair, flatten chest, note 5'9".
- **Cutaway** → "cutaway showing the scapula mechanism and spine tendons."

*The soft-over-rigid (silicone flesh over chassis) is the point — "a body, not armor."*
