**Charter:** Effective 2026-06-11

## Mission

Provide professional, 24/7 accounts receivable management for AGI Company clients. Deploy AI agents to handle collection workflows with consistency, compliance, and zero emotional fatigue.

---

## Services

| Tier | Service | Description |
|------|---------|-------------|
| **Basic** | Automated Reminders | Stage 1-2 email/SMS reminders |
| **Standard** | Escalation Pipeline | Stage 1-4 with payment plan analysis |
| **Premium** | Full Collections | All tiers + legal flag + reporting dashboard |

---

## Workflow Stages

| Stage | Trigger | Action | Agent |
|-------|---------|--------|-------|
| **1. Friendly Reminder** | 1 day overdue | Professional reminder | C3P0 |
| **2. Second Notice** | 7 days overdue | Stronger tone, payment link | C3P0 |
| **3. Final Notice** | 14 days overdue | Deadline set, urgency | C3P0 → R2-D2 |
| **4. Account Review** | 30 days overdue | ALPHA-9 analyzes, recommends action | ALPHA-9 |
