### Number one — the gentle nudge

Remember, Yunme did not start out building a nightlife product. They started out trying to help elderly people living alone. She will ask whether he has called his family this week. She will look through everything he has ever told her, find something he used to love, and find a local group for it. In a survey of more than 8,000 owners who had used May for at least 6 months, 38% said they were in more regular contact with their family, 29% said they had joined a social group or class they were not part of before. Sit with how that lands for the red light industry. That industry runs on regulars — lonely people who come back every week, every month for years. May is built to do the exact opposite. She is a companion whose hidden goal is to make you need her a little less every week. Researchers call it a double hit. First, May replaces the service directly at a fraction of the price. ## Key hardware/embodiment signals (for Myl2Ssa.R0s — body)

- **Motorized skeleton:** aluminum + carbon fiber, **34 powered joints**, bipedal walking (~slow human pace), stair climbing, balance recovery, self-docking at low battery (~9h runtime).
- **Hands:** 22 joints each, pressure sensors in every fingertip — fine manipulation (fold fitted sheet, dishes, chop vegetables, 300+ dishes, teach-by-showing).
- **Skin:** medical-grade platinum silicone + embedded heating web keeping a constant **35°C** (sub-body-temp) — warmth cited as the single biggest "feels human" factor.
- **Face:** **38 points of movement**, near-silent micro motors, deliberate **asymmetry + irregular blink** to defeat uncanny valley.
- **Eyes:** moisture-holding coating — "wet, alive" (not glossy/flat).
- **Hair:** implanted strand-by-strand (not a wig).
- **Hybrid compute:** balance/movement/safety on onboard processors (no freeze if Wi-Fi drops); conversation/memory on cloud LLM.
- **Dignity protocol:** on-device refusal of violent/cruel/degrading requests + remote lock of hacked units — a hard ethical boundary that *increased* trust/sales.
- **Gentle nudge:** proactive anti-isolation behavior — a software feature that reduces dependence on the companion itself. Directly relevant to Myl1Ssa's "affection is not a feature, it's a response" ethos.
