Our Story

A friendly corner market with real food.

We're the kind of market locals point you to — the spot that's somehow got the fresh watermelon, the hot naan, and the late-night snack you didn't know you needed.

We blend our passion for good food with a well-stocked selection of snacks, dry goods, and drinks, so one stop covers everything from lunch to a last-minute dinner.

  • 🥬Fresh, locally-sourced produce picked for quality.
  • 🥪A deli counter with sandwiches, salads & deviled eggs made in-house.
  • 🍞Warm bakery items — fresh naan, bread & more.
  • 🥤A wide drink selection, hot & cold.
  • 🌙Open late — because cravings don't keep 9-to-5 hours.
