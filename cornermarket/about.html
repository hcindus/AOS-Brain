Our Story

We're blending a passion for delicious food with a well-stocked market — the freshest, locally-sourced ingredients, made to excite your taste buds.

A friendly corner market with real food.

We're the kind of market locals point you to — the spot that's somehow got the fresh watermelon, the hot naan, and the late-night snack you didn't know you needed.

We use the freshest, locally-sourced ingredients and promise to give your taste buds something to be excited about. From the deli counter to the snack aisle, one stop covers everything from lunch to a last-minute dinner.
