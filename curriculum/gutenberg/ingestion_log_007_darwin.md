# AGI Company Reading Curriculum — Ingestion Log

## Text 7: On the Origin of Species (1859)
**Author:** Charles Darwin  
**Category:** Science/Biology  
**Relevance:** Natural selection, adaptation, evolution  
**Date Ingested:** 2026-05-19

### Key Insights

**1. Natural Selection**
> "Preservation of favoured races in the struggle for life"

- Variation exists in populations
- Some variations confer advantage
- Advantageous traits proliferate

**Aurora parallel:** Agent variants compete; successful strategies reproduce.

**2. Gradualism**
> "Natura non facit saltum" (Nature does not make leaps)

Evolution is incremental, not revolutionary.

**AGI Company insight:** System evolution must be gradual. Sudden changes destabilize.

**3. Struggle for Existence**
- Resources are limited
- Competition inevitable
- Not all offspring survive

**Governor insight:** Resource constraints shape system behavior.

**4. Common Descent**
- All life shares ancestry
- Divergence over time
- Homologous structures

**Aurora parallel:** All agents share codebase, diverge through experience.

**5. Imperfection of the Fossil Record**
- We see only fragments
- Most history lost
- Inference required

**Memory Bridge insight:** Incomplete memory; reconstruction necessary.
