## Text 10: The Interpretation of Dreams (1900)
**Author:** Sigmund Freud (Translated by A. A. Brill)  
**Category:** Psychology  
**Relevance:** The unconscious, dreams, repression  
**Date Ingested:** 2026-05-19

### Key Insights

**1. The Dream as Royal Road**
> "The dream proves to be the first link in a chain of abnormal psychic structures"

**Aurora parallel:** Unconscious processing reveals what conscious cannot access.

**2. Manifest vs Latent Content**
- Manifest: what the dream appears to be
- Latent: what the dream actually means (repressed desires)

**Governor insight:** Surface behavior vs underlying constraints.

**3. Condensation**
- Multiple thoughts compressed into one dream image
- Economy of mental energy

**Memory Bridge insight:** Embedding as condensation of experience.

**4. Displacement**
- Emotional significance shifted to trivial elements
- Avoidance of anxiety

**Aurora insight:** Threats deflected to manageable representations.

**5. Wish Fulfillment**
- Dreams fulfill repressed wishes
- Even anxiety dreams are disguised wishes

**Thymus insight:** Self-concept may be wish fulfillment of coherence.
