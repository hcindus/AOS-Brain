## Text 2: On the Origin of Species (1859)
**Author:** Charles Darwin  
**Category:** Science - Biology  
**Relevance:** Natural selection, adaptation, systems evolution  
**Date Ingested:** 2026-05-19

### Key Insights

**1. Natural Selection as Algorithm**
> "Natural Selection: its power compared with man's selection..."

Nature as an optimization process. **Aurora principle:** Systems can evolve without centralized control.

**2. Struggle for Existence**
> "The relation of organism to organism the most important of all relations."

Systems don't exist in isolation. **Immune system insight:** Self vs non-self requires context.

**3. Variation Under Domestication**
> "Principle of Selection anciently followed, its Effects."

Intentional selection creates outcomes faster than natural drift. **Governor insight:** Human guidance accelerates evolution.

**4. Geometrical Powers of Increase**
> "Rapid increase of naturalised animals and plants."

Unchecked growth is unsustainable. **Governor constraint:** `evolution_rate_limit` prevents runaway.

**5. Competition Universal**
> "Competition universal."

Resources are finite. **Thyroid insight:** Endocrine regulation as resource management.
