# AGI Company Reading Curriculum — Ingestion Log

## Text 3: The Art of War (c. 500 BCE)
**Author:** Sun Tzŭ  
**Category:** Business/Management — Strategy  
**Relevance:** Organization, planning, deception, adaptation  
**Date Ingested:** 2026-05-19

### Key Insights

**1. Laying Plans**
> "The general who wins a battle makes many calculations in his temple ere the battle is fought."

Planning before execution. **Governor principle:** Constraints must be established before action.

**2. Waging War**
> "There is no instance of a country having benefited from prolonged warfare."

Efficiency matters. **Thyroid insight:** Rapid state transitions conserve resources.

**3. Attack by Stratagem**
> "Supreme excellence consists in breaking the enemy's resistance without fighting."

Prevention > cure. **Immune system insight:** Quarantine toxic input before processing.

**4. Tactical Dispositions**
> "The good fighter is able to secure himself against defeat, but cannot make certain of defeating the enemy."

Defensive posture first. **Governor constraint:** Safety before capability.

**5. Energy**
> "The control of a large force is the same principle as the control of a few men."

Scalability through systems. **Aurora principle:** Fractal architecture.

**6. Weak Points and Strong**
> "You may advance and be absolutely irresistible, if you make for the enemy's weak points."

Focus resources. **Thyroid insight:** Allocate based on importance.

**7. Maneuvering**
> "Ponder and deliberate before you make a move."

OODA loop: Observe-Orient-Decide-Act. **Brain v4.5:** 7-region OODA.

**8. Variation of Tactics**> "Water shapes its course according to the nature of the ground over which it flows."

Adaptation without losing identity. **Thymus insight:** Learn "self" first, then adapt.

**9. The Army on the March**
> "We are not fit to lead an army on the march unless we are familiar with the face of the country."

Context awareness. **Cortex 3D:** Spatial processing.

**10. Terrain**
> "Ground on which we can only be saved from destruction by fighting is called 'desperate.'"

Avoid desperation. **Governor constraint:** Never enter states with no exit.

**11. The Nine Situations**
> "The skillful tactician may be likened to the shuai-jan."

(Snake that strikes with both head and tail simultaneously.) **Immune system:** Multi-layer defense.

**12. The Attack by Fire**
> "The enlightened ruler lays his plans well ahead."

Preparation. **Persistence v1.0:** State survives restarts.

**13. The Use of Spies**
> "Spies are a most important element in water, because on them depends an army's ability to move."

Intelligence gathering. **WhiteCell:** Patrols detect threats before they arrive.
