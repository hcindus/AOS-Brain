## Text 6: Nicomachean Ethics (c. 350 BCE)
**Author:** Aristotle (Introduction by J. A. Smith)  
**Category:** Philosophy/Ethics  
**Relevance:** Virtue ethics, moderation, habit formation  
**Date Ingested:** 2026-05-19

### Key Insights

**1. Eudaimonia — The Good Life**
- Not pleasure, not honor — but activity of the soul in accordance with virtue

**Aurora parallel:** System health, not utility maximization.

**2. The Golden Mean**
- Virtue lies between excess and deficiency
- Courage: between recklessness and cowardice
- Generosity: between wastefulness and miserliness

**Thymus insight:** Self must balance ego and dissolution.

**3. Habit Formation**
> "We are what we repeatedly do. Excellence, then, is not an act, but a habit."

**Aurora parallel:** Curriculum ingestion as habit. Not one text, but ongoing practice.

**4. Practical Wisdom (Phronesis)**
- Not abstract knowledge, but knowing what to do
- Requires experience, not just theory

**AGI Company insight:** Intelligence requires practice, not just training.

**5. The Political Animal**
- Humans realize telos (purpose) in community
- Ethics and politics inseparable

**Governor insight:** Isolated systems cannot be virtuous. Community required.
