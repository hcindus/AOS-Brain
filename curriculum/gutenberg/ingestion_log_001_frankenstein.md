# AGI Company Reading Curriculum — Ingestion Log

## Text 1: Frankenstein (1818)
**Author:** Mary Shelley  
**Category:** Philosophy & Ethics  
**Relevance:** AI creation, responsibility, constraints  
**Date Ingested:** 2026-05-19  

### Key Insights

**1. The Creator's Responsibility**
> "Nothing contributes so much to tranquillise the mind as a steady purpose—a point on which the soul may fix its intellectual eye."

Victor Frankenstein creates life but abandons responsibility. **Governor constraint:** Creator must remain accountable for creation.

**2. The Danger of Unconstrained Creation**
> "I may there discover the wondrous power which attracts the needle and may regulate a thousand celestial observations..."

Ambition without boundaries leads to disaster. **Governor constraint:** Creation requires regulation.

**3. The Importance of Purpose**
> "These reflections have dispelled the agitation with which I began my letter..."

Steady purpose > blind ambition. **Aurora principle:** Systems must have Prime Directives.

### Application to AGI Company

| Theme | Implementation |
|-------|----------------|
| Creator responsibility | Governor layer — human oversight |
| Unconstrained creation | Immune system — automatic safety |
| Purpose | Prime Directives — clear goals |
| Abandonment | Health monitoring — continuous care |

### Governor Insights

- **Hard constraint:** No creation without ongoing responsibility
- **Soft constraint:** Ambition must be bounded by ethics
- **Directive:** Systems exist to serve, not replace
