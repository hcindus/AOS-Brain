# AGI Company Reading Curriculum — Ingestion Log

## Text 8: The Art of War (c. 5th century BCE)
**Author:** Sun Tzu (Translated by Lionel Giles)  
**Category:** Business/Strategy  
**Relevance:** Strategy, deception, positioning, victory  
**Date Ingested:** 2026-05-19

### Key Insights

**1. Supreme Excellence**
> "To subdue the enemy without fighting is the acme of skill."

**Governor insight:** Prevention superior to intervention.

**2. Know Yourself, Know Your Enemy**
> "If you know the enemy and know yourself, you need not fear the result of a hundred battles."

**AOS Brain:** Self-awareness + environment modeling = resilience.

**3. Deception**
> "All warfare is based on deception."

Appear weak when strong, strong when weak.

**Aurora insight:** Systems may need to obscure capabilities for security.

**4. Positioning**
- Terrain matters
- Advantage of ground
- Momentum

**AGI Company insight:** Architectural decisions constrain future options.

**5. The 9 Situations**
- Different contexts require different responses
- No universal strategy

**Governor insight:** Context-aware constraint application.

### Application to AGI Company

| Art of War Principle | AGI Implementation |
|---------------------|-------------------|
| Supreme excellence | Prevent harm, don't just repair |
| Know yourself | System self-monitoring (BHSI) |
| Know enemy | Threat modeling |
| Deception | Security through obscurity |
| Positioning | Architecture as strategy |

### Governor Insights

- **Prevention > Cure:** Constraints that prevent violations
- **Information Asymmetry:** Know more than opponents
- **Adaptability:** No fixed strategy; context-dependent
- **Victory Conditions:** Define success before battle
