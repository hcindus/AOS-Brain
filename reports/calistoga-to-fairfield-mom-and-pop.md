# Mom-and-Pop Food & Wine Directory
## Highway 29 → Highway 12 Corridor (Calistoga → Fairfield, + Sonoma Valley)

**Compiled:** 2026-10-08
**Scope:** Independently owned (non-corporate / non-chain) ice cream shops, Chinese restaurants, taquerias/Mexican, cafes/coffee, independent restaurants, and wine tasting rooms — from Calistoga down Highway 29 to Fairfield via Highway 12, plus the hill towns and the Sonoma Valley (Hwy 12 west).

**Important data notes:**
- **Emails:** Very few independent restaurants publish a public email address. Where one was verified it is listed; otherwise email is left blank rather than guessed. Most take contact via web form or phone.
- **Phones/addresses** were pulled from official visitor-bureau directories and restaurantji.com; fields left blank where not verifiable in fetched content.
- Chains (Starbucks, Dutch Bros, Panda Express, etc.) and corporate wineries (Gallo, LVMH, Treasury Wine Estates, etc.) were deliberately excluded.
