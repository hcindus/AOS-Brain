## Rationale

**1 oz & 1.5 oz (70 units):** These are the bread-and-butter. Every bar needs them. Most common pour sizes in the US. Higher qtys = better per-unit shipping + early reorder buffer.

**2 oz (20 units):** Used for heavier pours, craft cocktail programs. Lower volume but important to have on hand.

**Wine Pourer (12 units):** Wine programs are typically 3-5 bottles per restaurant. 12 covers initial demos + 1-2 installations.

**Variety Packs (4 units):** Good for new accounts who want to test multiple sizes. 4 packs = 2 display units + 2 sellable.

**Cleaning Kits (8 units):** High attach rate — sell one with every install. Recurring revenue from brush/cap replacements.
