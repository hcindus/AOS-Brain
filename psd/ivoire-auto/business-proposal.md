# Business Proposal: ICON4x4 × Ivoire Auto Partnership

## Executive Summary

**Opportunity:** Introduce ICON4x4's handcrafted luxury 4x4 vehicles to the Côte d'Ivoire market through an exclusive distribution partnership with Ivoire Auto.

**Parties:**
- **ICON4x4** (Los Angeles, CA) - Manufacturer of custom, hand-built 4x4 vehicles
- **Ivoire Auto** (Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire) - Exclusive distributor for West African markets
- **Performance Supply Depot LLC** - Facilitating partner & logistics coordinator

## Côte d'Ivoire Market Opportunity

### Market Size
- Population: 27 million
- GDP Growth: 6-7% annually (one of Africa's fastest-growing)
- Abidjan: Economic capital, 5+ million residents
- Emerging luxury goods market

### Vehicle Market
- Strong preference for robust 4x4 vehicles (road conditions)
- Growing affluent class seeking luxury imports
- Limited local competition in hand-built custom segment
- Status symbol culture favors unique, imported vehicles

### Entry Strategy
1. **Phase 1:** Import 3-5 showcase vehicles
2. **Phase 2:** Establish service center in Abidjan
3. **Phase 3:** Limited production runs for West African market

## Partnership Structure

### Ivoire Auto Responsibilities
- **Exclusive Distribution:** Côte d'Ivoire, Ghana, Senegal
- **Showroom:** Prime location in Abidjan (Cocody or Plateau)
- **Marketing:** Local luxury vehicle shows, diplomatic events
- **Service:** Technical training for ICON4x4-certified mechanics
- **Sales:** Target 12-24 units annually by Year 3

### ICON4x4 Responsibilities
- **Vehicle Supply:** Priority allocation for Ivoire Auto
- **Technical Support:** Remote diagnostics, parts supply chain
- **Brand Protection:** Quality control, brand guidelines
- **Training:** Initial staff training in Los Angeles

### Performance Supply Depot Role
- **Logistics:** International shipping, customs clearance
- **Financing:** Letters of credit, payment processing
- **Documentation:** Import/export compliance
- **Relationship Management:** Ongoing partnership coordination
