## Proposal Structure (VSL Format)

### 1. Pain Agitation
**Goal:** Describe their pain better than they can

**Formula:**
- "Running a [business type] means..."
- "When you're dealing with [pain point], it's not just inconvenient..."
- "Every minute spent [dealing with issue] is a minute not spent [growing business]"

### 2. Solution Presentation
**Goal:** Position PSD as the obvious answer

**Formula:**
- "Here's how Performance Supply Depot solves this..."
- "✓ [Specific benefit related to their pain]"
- "✓ [Another benefit]"
- "We become your supply partner, not just another vendor"

### 3. Product Recommendations
**Goal:** Specific products matched to their needs

**Template:**
```
Based on your [X locations] and [Y monthly volume], I recommend:

• [Product 1] - [Quantity] - [Why this matches their need]
• [Product 2] - [Quantity] - [Why this solves their pain]
• [Product 3] - [Quantity] - [Added value]
```

### 4. Investment
**Goal:** Clear pricing within their budget

**Format:**
```
Monthly Supply Investment: $X,XXX
✓ Includes free delivery
✓ Net 30 payment terms
✓ No long-term contract
```

### 5. Social Proof
**Goal:** "Someone like me got results"

**Formula:**
- "[Similar customer] had the same challenge with [specific pain]"
- "After switching to PSD: [specific result]"
- "[Quote from customer if available]"

### 6. Call to Action
**Goal:** Clear next step

**Examples:**
- "Ready to never run out of supplies again?"
- "Let's get your first delivery scheduled"
- "Are you ready to join the team?"

## Talk Track Structure

### Opening Hook
"Thanks for taking the time today, [Name]. I spent some time looking into [Company] and I've got to say, I'm excited about what you're building."

"Before I show you what I put together, I want to make sure I understand your situation correctly..."

### Pain Questions
- "From what I heard, you're dealing with [pain point]. Is that right?"
- "How is that impacting your day-to-day operations?"
- "What does it cost you when [specific pain scenario]?"
- "What have you tried to solve it so far?"

### Solution Walkthrough
"Here's what I put together for you..."

[Screenshare proposal]

"I looked at your [specific research detail], and I thought about what [similar customer] went through. They were dealing with the same thing."

### Closing
"Does this feel like the right solution for [Company]?"

"Are you ready to join the team?"

"Let's get you set up with your first delivery."

## Pull Prompting Method

**When to use:** Complex deals with unclear needs

**Process:**
1. Define outcome: "Create proposal that converts [Company]"
2. AI asks questions: "What questions do you need answered?"
3. Human answers: Provide context via voice/text
4. AI synthesizes: Builds proposal from gathered intel
5. Output: Complete proposal brief
