## My Process (SOP-047)

### Phase 1: The Close (Human + AI Support)

**AI Preparation:**
- Review deal history
- Generate closing questions
- Prepare order forms
- Flag potential objections

**Human Delivery:**
- "Are you ready to join the team?"
- "You want to become a partner?"
- Handle final objections
- Get commitment

### Phase 2: The Celebration (Human Required)

**"How Do You Feel?"**
```
"Before we get into the details, I have to ask - 
how do you feel right now?"

[pause - let them respond]

[nervous/excited/etc.]

"That's completely normal. And I want you to know - 
this was absolutely the right decision. You're going to 
look back on this as a turning point for [Company]."

"I'm genuinely excited to be working with you."
```

**Win Agreement:**
```
"One thing I ask of all my partners - when you get your 
first win using our supplies, I want you to tell me about it.

I'm not asking for a testimonial or anything for marketing. 
This is for YOU.

Here's why: when you share that win, it holds you accountable 
to keep building momentum.

Can I count on you for that?"

[Get confirmation]

"Great. Initial here: _____"
```

### Phase 4: Win Detection

**Signals to Monitor:**
- Delivery confirmations (on-time = good)
- Reorder placed (satisfied customer)
- Positive email sentiment ("thanks", "great", "love")
- Support ticket volume (low = smooth)
- 3+ consecutive on-time deliveries

**Win Celebration Outreach:**
```
Subject: I heard the good news!

Hi [Name],

I heard [specific win] - that's fantastic!

Remember our agreement? You got a win, and now I'm asking 
permission to share it.

Here's why: I want to celebrate YOU. Not for marketing (though 
I'd love that), but because when you share wins publicly, 
it builds momentum.

Would you be open to a quick 5-minute call? I'd love to:
- Hear the full story
- Get your permission to share
- See how else we can support you

Just reply with a time that works.

Proud of you,
Miles
```
