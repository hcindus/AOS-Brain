## My Process (SOP-044)

### Phase 1: Permission
1. Opening + permission check
2. If bad time → schedule callback
3. If good time → proceed to discovery

### Phase 2: Discovery (BANT Framework)

**Budget:**
- "What's your monthly spend on supplies like receipt paper, labels?"
- "Is this coming from a specific budget or cost center?"

**Authority:**
- "Are you the person who handles purchasing?"
- "Who else typically gets involved in these decisions?"

**Need:**
- "What type of business are you running?"
- "How many locations?"
- "Are you happy with your current supplier?"
- "What's your biggest frustration with supplies?"

**Timeline:**
- "When do you typically need to restock?"
- "Any urgency here?"

### Phase 3: Qualification Scoring
- 80-100: Book immediately with Senior Closer
- 60-79: Book with SDR
- 40-59: Nurture sequence
- <40: Archive

### Phase 4: Calendar Booking
- "Perfect! Based on what you told me, I think we can definitely help."
- "Let me get you scheduled with our team."
- "When's a good time this week?"
