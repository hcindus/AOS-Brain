# SOP-049 — The Consultative Approach

**Source:** *The Consultative Approach: Partnering for Results!* — Virginia LaGrossa (1998)
**KPI:** 40%+ conversion rate · 90%+ client satisfaction · sell the *result*, not the box.

---

## Process

1. **Partner, don't pitch.** Position yourself as a trusted advisor — never a transactional vendor. The client buys a partner, not a product.

2. **Ask before you answer.** Open-ended questions first. Understand the client's business, pain points, and goals before proposing a single thing.

3. **Diagnose, then prescribe.** Match the solution to the *actual* need. If a $0 fix solves it (bent bill guide vs. new drawer), say so — trust compounds.

4. **Present the result.** Lead with the outcome (margin saved, uptime, throughput), not the spec sheet. "What this does for you" beats "what this is."

5. **Handle objections with empathy.** Feel → Felt → Found. Acknowledge, relate, then share how others found a path.

6. **Close as a partner.** The goal is a result for the client, not a commission for you. Under-promise, over-deliver.

---

## Rules

- Never pitch before diagnosing the need.
- Lead with outcome; features are supporting detail.
- No invented statistics — specific and honest, always.
- If the client's need is small, sell them the small thing.
- Every interaction leaves the client better informed than you found them.
