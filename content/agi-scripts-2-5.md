## Video 2: "I Fired My Cloud Bill, Not My People"
- **HOOK:** "I cut my AI bill from $2,000 a month to fifty bucks — and didn't lay off a single agent."
- **TENSION:** "Cloud AI vendors told me local models are 'toys.' That I'd never scale. That I'd need a GPU cluster." → show the $2,000 invoice, the vendor pitch decks.
- **PAYOFF:** "66 agents. Support, sales, billing, research — all running on a box that fits in a drawer. No per-token meter. No surprise bill."
- **CTA:** "Build your own for $499/month. Bring your worst workflow first, free."

## Video 3: "This Agent Answers My Phone While I Sleep"
- **HOOK:** "My receptionist never sleeps, never takes a day off, and costs $499 a month."
- **TENSION:** "Missed calls were killing my pipeline — every ring is a customer walking away. Hiring a human 24/7 costs $3,000+." → show the missed-call log.
- **PAYOFF:** "GREET answers every call, qualifies the lead, books the calendar — while I'm asleep. It's not a chatbot; it's the front desk."
- **CTA:** "Your own 24/7 front desk from $499/month."

## Video 4: "What 66 Agents Cost vs What SaaS Would"
- **HOOK:** "Sixty-six employees for fifty bucks a month. Here's the receipt."
- **TENSION:** "Not people — agents. And the same workload on a SaaS stack? $1,500–2,000/month, plus a per-seat fee for every 'employee.'" → side-by-side cost sheet.
- **PAYOFF:** "Local models. No seats. No tokens. The whole company runs on less than one cloud agent."
- **CTA:** "See the numbers. Bring your worst workflow, free."

## Video 5: "My Sales Agent Landed a Client on a $99 Box"
- **HOOK:** "This closed a deal — running on a $99 box, not a $10,000 rig."
- **TENSION:** "Everyone assumes serious AI needs serious hardware. A GPU cluster. A data center. A six-figure contract." → the doubt.
- **PAYOFF:** "Jane qualified, followed up, and closed — from a machine you could put in a backpack. Fractional-horsepower AI, doing real work."
- **CTA:** "Get your own closer from $999/month."

## CTA / offer (every video ends with one)
- Entry: **$499/mo** · mid: **$999/mo** · enterprise: **$1,999–3,999/mo**
- Lead magnet: **"bring your worst workflow"** free trial (this is the lead-scoring hook)
- Always name price + challenge. Never end on a philosophy.

## Production
- Completed runs only, narrated over. No live demos.
- Bolt-style glowing captions on the money lines.
- Post YouTube → TikTok → Instagram; seed into founder/AI-ops Slack/Discord/LinkedIn.
