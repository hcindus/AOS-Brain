## CHANNEL 1 — AGI Company (reshaped)

**One-sentence thesis:** Prove a small local model does real work cheaper — then sell the *outcome*, not the hardware.

### Positioning
- Anchor (used as *closing line only*, never the opener): *"We do it great, not so-so."*
- The differentiator is **fractional-horsepower AI** — but framed as a *result*, not a mechanism.
- Never lead with "2GB vs $10K GPU." Lead with *"this agent resolved 1,000 tickets last month."*

### The hook (contrast, outcome-first)
- *"I fired my $5K/mo support stack for this."*
- *"This agent ran my billing for a month on a $99 box."*
- *"What my old SaaS cost me in a year, this agent costs in a week."*

### Content format — COMPLETED case studies (not live demos)
- Show a **finished, successful** run — never a live demo (one visible error = credibility gone).
- Raw, unedited, on-device footage (this is the white space competitors avoid).
- 3-part structure: hook (the result) → tension (the old expensive way) → payoff (the cheap win + the numbers).

### Offer / CTA (fixes the "what am I buying?" hole)
- Explicit: *"We run your support for $X/mo."*
- Lead magnet: free trial / sandbox / **"bring your worst ticket"** challenge.
- Lead-scoring rubric to define "qualified" (ICP: SMB ops owner, has a support/billing workflow, budget ≥ $500/mo).

### KPI (lead quality, not reach)
- 20 qualified leads + 10 booked demos in 90 days.
- Pilot: 10 videos, $0 ad spend — but prioritize 3 *great* case studies over 10 mediocre.

### Cadence & distribution
- 3–4 videos/week, YouTube Shorts → TikTok → Instagram.
- Cross-post into founder/AI-ops communities (Slack/Discord/LinkedIn) for the first 500 views/video.

## CHANNEL 2 — Performance Supply Depot (pivoted)

**One-sentence thesis:** Capture high-intent search demand that already exists — don't try to create it on TikTok.

### The pivot
- **Kill the Shorts play.** POS buyers search Google, not Shorts.
- Focus all $1,500 + 35 hrs on **one high-margin SKU: Capton 1.5oz pourer.**

### Why Capton pourer only
- Weak SERPs (mostly thin Amazon/eBay listings) = beatable.
- High margin (30–50%) = ROAS-positive is realistic.
- Concrete pain point = emotional hook ("your bartender is pouring away profit").

### Execution
1. **Paid first:** Google Ads, exact/phrase match + negative keywords on `capton pourer 1.5oz`, `capton pourer for 750ml bottle`, `capton pourer with speed pourer`.
2. **Content second:** 5–8 buying guides / comparison posts fed by ad search-query data ("Capton 1.5oz vs 2oz", "how much a bar loses to overpouring").
3. Drop commodity terms (thermal paper, ribbons) — CPC $1.50–3 vs $20–40 AOV = no ROAS.

### KPI (realistic)
- ROAS-positive paid on Capton pourer (prove it before scaling).
- Organic: 300–600/mo (not the fantasy 1,000).
- Skip domain-authority/backlink ambitions in the 90-day window.
