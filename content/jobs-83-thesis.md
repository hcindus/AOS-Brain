## 1. The Core Thesis (our positioning)

**"We have an opportunity to do it great or to do it so-so."**

Jobs's entire talk is one argument: the new technology (PCs) is *inevitable* — it's going to be in every home, school, and office whether it's beautiful or junk. The only real choice is **great vs so-so**. That's exactly our stance on AI agents.

**Inevitability frame:** "Society and computers are out on a first date in the eighties."
→ Ours: "Society and AI agents are out on a first date right now."

---

## 2. The Killer Mapping: "Fractional-Horsepower Computing" → "Fractional-Horsepower AI"

This is the single most valuable idea in the talk, and it maps *directly* to what AGI Company already does.

**Jobs's analogy (the electric motor):**
1. Big motor → only cost-justified for huge factories
2. Shaft + belts → shared across 15–20 workstations
3. **Fractional-horsepower motor** → horsepower delivered exactly where needed → 55 motors in every household

**Jobs's punchline:** "The reason Apple exists is because we stumbled onto fractional-horsepower computing five years before anybody else."

**Our translation (AI):**
1. Big cloud model (GPT/Claude) → only "cost-justified" for huge centralized use
2. API sharing → shared across many apps
3. **Fractional-horsepower AI** → small local models (tinyllama, gemma2:2b, Mort_II) running *exactly where the work is* → an agent in every business, every counter, every device

**This is literally our stack** (see TOOLS.md): we run tinyllama/gemma2:2b on a Pi, Mort_II for voice. We are the "fractional-horsepower AI" company five years early. This is our "we exist because" story.

## 3. Jobs's Own Storytelling Structure (a masterclass in the "wisdom" bucket)

Jobs doesn't just *talk about* design — his talk *is* structured like a viral short. Reverse-engineer it:

1. **HOOK** — disarm + intrigue in 10 sec: "They paid me $60, so I wore a tie." / "How many of you are older than 36? You were born pre-computer."
2. **DEMYSTIFY** — make the intimidating thing simple: "What is a computer? Gears and pistons replaced by electrons." (→ "What is an AI agent? Rules and memory, running a million steps a second.")
3. **ANALOGY** — the electric motor / fractional horsepower. Ground the abstract in the concrete.
4. **INEVITABILITY + STAKES** — "We're going to sell 10M computers whether they look like shit or great. It doesn't cost more to make it great."
5. **CALL TO ACTION** — "I need your help. We really, really need your help."

**Pattern:** Hook → simplify → analogy → inevitability+stakes → call-to-action. This is the "wisdom/vision" content structure to pair with Mathis's "story" structure.

## 5. The Deepest Idea (our "north star")

Jobs on software: **"Computer programming captures the underlying principles of an experience — not the experience itself."**

Then the Aristotle example: *"Maybe someday, after the person is dead and gone, we can ask this machine, 'What would Aristotle have said?'"*

→ This is *exactly* what an **agent manifest / persona** is. We're not building chatbots; we're capturing the underlying principles of a role (Miles, Patricia, GREET) so the machine can embody it. **We build the thing Jobs was dreaming about 40 years ago.**

---

## 6. Distribution / Privacy insight (maps to our local-first stance)

Jobs's answer to the privacy concern: **"Distribute that intelligence... that can turn all this information into knowledge."**

→ Our local-model / sovereignty angle: instead of centralizing intelligence in a few cloud giants, we *distribute* it — an agent that runs on *your* hardware, *your* data, *your* rules. This is the ethical/political frame behind fractional-horsepower AI.
