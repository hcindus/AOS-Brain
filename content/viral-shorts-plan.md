# Viral Shorts Plan — AGI Company & Performance Supply Depot
**Built on Mathis Bolt's framework (Hook → Tension → Payoff)**
**Date:** 2026-08-23

---

## CHANNEL 1: AGI Company

**Niche:** "How AI agents actually run a business" — autonomous operations, not hype.
**Positioning:** We ARE the thing everyone's scared of, but shown as a builder, not a threat.
**Content buckets:** Wisdom (how it works) · Coldest moments (the agent does the impossible) · Wholesome (the human freed from busywork) · Trending (react to AI news with receipts).

### Posting cadence
- 3–4 videos/week (quality over quantity — Mathis hit 250M on 9/month)
- One platform-first: YouTube Shorts (best RPM) → repost TikTok + Instagram same day

### Production workflow (Bolt-adapted)
1. Screen-record the agent/build doing the thing (no cinematic setup needed — real footage wins)
2. AI-upscale/clean audio
3. Draft story in Premiere (or DaVinci — free) — 3-part structure
4. "Boltify": glowing captions on the key line, bold color grade, a 1-word watermark
5. AI (Google AI Studio) to find the best 3-sec moment for the hook

### First 5 hooks (with tension → payoff)

**1. "We fired our customer service team."**
- HOOK: freeze on an angry-looking "Wait, what?" face + the cold line on screen.
- TENSION: show the flood of tickets, the fear, "everyone told us this was stupid."
- PAYOFF: the AI voice agent resolves a real call live, customer says "that was fast."

**2. "This agent made $X while I slept."**
- HOOK: a dashboard number ticking up in the dark.
- TENSION: "nobody believes this is real" → show the setup, the fear of it breaking.
- PAYOFF: the morning reveal + proof of the trades/leads.

**3. "I built a company that runs without me."**
- HOOK: empty desk + "Day 30: still nobody here."
- TENSION: list everything it's doing (support, billing, docs) while skepticism builds.
- PAYOFF: the single dashboard that runs it all.

**4. "The robot that pours a perfect drink" (crossover w/ PSD)**
- HOOK: slow-mo perfect pour, no spill.
- TENSION: "bar owners waste $X/year on overpours" + the stakes.
- PAYOFF: the measured, exact pour → owner's margins recovered.

**5. "This is why everyone's scared of AI"**
- HOOK: bold claim + a sharp cut.
- TENSION: show what it does faster/better than a human, the uncomfortable truth.
- PAYOFF: reframe — "it's a tool, and here's who's using it to win."

## CHANNEL 2: Performance Supply Depot (PSD)

**Niche:** Restaurant/bar/retail equipment — satisfying, "coldest moments," and "you didn't know this" education.
**Positioning:** The gear behind the counter, shown as satisfying + money-saving.
**Content buckets:** Wholesome (small business wins) · Coldest moments (the hack that saves money) · Wisdom (did you know your POS/equipment) · Satisfying (pours, drawer slides, print speed).

### Posting cadence
- 3–4 videos/week, same YouTube→TikTok→Instagram flow.
- Lean hard on satisfying/visual content (PSD is a visual product, not a face brand).

### Production workflow
1. Shoot real product close-ups (cash drawer snap, Capton pour, receipt printer speed) — phone is fine
2. Bold captions on the "money line," punchy color grade
3. 3-part structure: satisfying action → the hidden cost/problem → the payoff

### First 5 hooks (with tension → payoff)

**1. "Your bartender is pouring away your profit."**
- HOOK: a drink overpoured, slow-mo, money literally down the drain.
- TENSION: the math — "$X lost per bottle, per shift" + the pain of thin margins.
- PAYOFF: the Capton 1.5oz pourer → exact measure every time → "here's what you get back."

**2. "The cash drawer that changed how this shop closes."**
- HOOK: drawer snapping open/shut, satisfying click.
- TENSION: end-of-day counting anxiety, the theft risk.
- PAYOFF: the exact drawer model + the calm close-out.

**3. "This receipt printer is stupidly fast."**
- HOOK: a receipt flying out in a blur.
- TENSION: "slow printers kill your line at rush hour" + the wait cost.
- PAYOFF: the speed test vs. a generic printer.

**4. "We sent a POS system to a struggling cafe."**
- HOOK: the "before" — chaos, handwritten tickets.
- TENSION: the owner's story, the fear of upgrading.
- PAYOFF: the "after" — clean orders, faster service, saved hours.

**5. "The $0 hack that cuts your overpour waste."**
- HOOK: "stop throwing money away" cold-open.
- TENSION: the waste numbers, the skepticism ("it's just a pourer").
- PAYOFF: the demo + the exact ROI math.
