# Capton 1.5oz Pourer — Google Ads Keyword Set + Buying Guide
**Channel:** Performance Supply Depot (pivoted to search capture)
**Scope:** ONE SKU only — Capton 1.5oz bottle-top pourer (SKU CAP-150-15OZ, $49.00)

---

## 1. Google Ads Keyword Set

### Campaign structure
Single SKU, exact + phrase match, tight ad group. Bid on **long-tail modifiers** where competitors' relevance drops (pos-depot.com has higher domain authority but weak ad relevance on specifics).

### Exact-match (high intent, bid highest)
- `[capton pourer 1.5 oz]`
- `[capton 1.5 oz bottle top pourer]`
- `[capton pourer for 750ml bottle]`
- `[capton pourer with speed pourer]`
- `[buy capton pourer 1.5oz]`

### Phrase-match (medium intent)
- `"capton pourer 1.5oz"`
- `"capton 1.5 oz pourer"`
- `"capton bottle pourer"`
- `"1.5 oz liquor pourer"`
- `"portion control pourer 1.5 oz"`

### Negative keywords (filter junk / "how-to" / wrong intent)
- `-parts` `-replacement` `-repair` `-how to` `-review` `-free` `-2 oz` `-wine` `-cleaning` `-used` `-ebay` `-amazon`

### Why this set (from council)
- "Capton pourer 1.5oz" has weak SERPs (thin Amazon/eBay listings) → beatable.
- 30–50% margin → ROAS-positive at $0.50–$1.50 CPC.
- Avoid broad "POS supplies" / "thermal paper" (CPC $1.50–3 vs $20–40 AOV = no ROAS).

## 2. First Buying Guide (content asset)

**Title:** *Capton Pourer 1.5oz vs 2oz: Which Pourer Stops Your Bar Losing Money?*
**Target keywords:** "capton pourer 1.5 oz", "1.5 oz vs 2 oz pourer", "how much do bars lose overpouring"

### Outline
1. **H2: The overpour tax** — hook with the dollar math: a bar overpouring 0.5oz/drink at $X/drink loses $Y/night, $Z/year. (This is the emotional hook that converts.)
2. **H2: Why pourer size is the whole game** — 1.5oz = standard shot; 2oz = ~33% overpour. Explain portion control in plain terms.
3. **H2: Capton 1.5oz vs 2oz — side by side** — table: pour volume, per-bottle servings, monthly cost, who each is for.
4. **H2: The math on a single bottle** — "a 750ml bottle = ~17 pours at 1.5oz vs ~12.6 at 2oz → X more paid drinks per bottle."
5. **H2: Who should buy the 1.5oz** — high-volume bars, cocktail programs, owners protecting margins.
6. **H2: FAQ (schema-marked)** — "Does it leak?" "Dishwasher safe?" "Fits all bottle necks?" (targets long-tail voice/feature queries)
7. **CTA:** buy button + "free shipping over $X" + link to Capton variety pack for upsell.

### Note
- Build this *after* the first 2 weeks of ad search-query data (let real queries dictate the exact H2s/FAQ).
- Add Product + FAQPage schema (site already has the pattern).

## 3. AOV / free-shipping math (the critical correction)

A single Capton pourer = **$49.00**, but free shipping kicks in at **$500**. A one-unit order never clears the bar, and paid clicks on a $49 item with thin margin + shipping cost = no ROAS.

**Fix — bundle/upsell to protect AOV:**
- Ad landing page leads with the **Capton Variety Pack (12-pack, $558.00)** → clears $500 free-shipping automatically.
- Or a multi-unit offer ("bar kit": 6× 1.5oz pourers + cleaning kit) to push AOV over the threshold.
- Bid on bundle-intent terms too: `capton variety pack`, `capton 12 pack`, `capton pourer wholesale`.

### 90-day sequence
1. **Week 1–2:** launch exact/phrase ads, collect search-query report.
2. **Week 3–4:** publish the buying guide fed by real queries; add FAQ schema; test Variety-Pack landing page vs single-SKU.
3. **Week 5+:** optimize bids, prune negatives, scale only ROAS-positive terms and bundle offers.
