## 1. Executive Summary

The MS-Connect conference revealed significant opportunities for Performance Supply Depot to expand its POS solution portfolio, particularly on the West Coast where Pecan POS is actively seeking dealers. The conference highlighted three key market trends:

1. **AI Integration** is becoming table stakes (AI Bunny, AI dashboards, automated workflows)
2. **Pricing Pressure** is brutal - vendors are moving to flexible/subscription models to compete
3. **West Coast Dealer Gap** represents a first-mover opportunity for established providers

AM HUD Supply's vision of becoming the "go-to solution provider" aligns well with the vendor opportunities identified, particularly the focus on small-to-medium businesses with low-complexity, high-quality alternatives.

**Key Recommendation:** Prioritize Pecan POS West Coast dealer relationship and Diner Daddy competitive positioning against Toast.
